Q2: Are payment plans available?

Yes, we understand that dental care can be a significant investment. We offer flexible payment options and accept various insurance plans to help make your treatment affordable.

We generally recommend scheduling a routine dental check-up and cleaning every six months. However, the frequency may vary depending on your individual oral health needs.

Getting started is simple. You can call us at (801) 855-0555 to schedule an initial consultation. During this visit, we will discuss your dental concerns, review your medical history, and create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs.
