Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of dental services does Stonehaven Dental Lehi offer?

We provide a comprehensive range of dental services, including preventative care (exams, cleanings, X-rays), restorative treatments (fillings, crowns, bridges, dentures), endodontics (root canals), periodontics (gum disease treatment), and cosmetic dentistry (teeth whitening, veneers, Invisalign).

Q2: How often should I schedule dental checkups at Stonehaven Dental Lehi?

We generally recommend scheduling a comprehensive dental checkup and professional cleaning every six months. However, the frequency may vary depending on your individual oral health needs, which our dentist will determine during your examination.

Q3: Does Stonehaven Dental Lehi offer emergency dental care?

While we primarily schedule appointments, we understand dental emergencies can happen. Please call our office at (801) 855-0555 during regular business hours. For after-hours emergencies, we can provide you with the contact information for a dentist who offers emergency services.

Q4: Are payment plans available for dental services at Stonehaven Dental Lehi?

Yes, we understand that dental care costs can be significant. We offer various payment options and work with multiple insurance providers to help make your treatment affordable. Please inquire about payment plans when you schedule your appointment.

Q5: What should I bring to my first dental appointment at Stonehaven Dental Lehi?

For your first visit, please bring your valid insurance card, a list of any medications you take (including dosages), your photo ID, and your previous dental X-rays if you have them. If you have any specific questions or concerns beforehand, feel free to call our office at (801) 855-0555.
