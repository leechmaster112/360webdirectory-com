Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What is axe throwing?

A: Axe throwing is a recreational activity where participants learn to throw axes at a target board. It's a fun, social, and often competitive pastime enjoyed by people of all ages and skill levels.

Q2: Is axe throwing safe?

A: Absolutely! At Axe Throwing Toms River NJ, safety is our number one priority. We provide comprehensive safety instructions, use high-quality, blunt-tipped axes, and ensure our targets and facility are designed for a secure experience.

Q3: Can beginners participate?

A: Yes, definitely! We welcome beginners. Our staff will teach you the fundamentals of grip, stance, and release during a pre-session briefing. With practice, you'll quickly improve your accuracy.

Q4: What are the age restrictions?

A: Guests must be at least 18 years old to participate independently. Minors aged 16-17 may participate with a parent or guardian present and signing a waiver. Children under 16 are not permitted to participate at our Toms River location.

Q5: How do I book a session?

A: You can easily book your axe throwing session online by visiting our website https://axethrowingtomsrivernj.com. You can also call us at (732) 555-4812 to make a reservation, especially for private parties or groups.
