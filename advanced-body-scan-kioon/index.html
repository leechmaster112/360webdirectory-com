Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of scans does Advanced Body Scan offer at its Bellaire location?

A1: Advanced Body Scan at 4460 Bissonnet St, Bellaire, TX 77401 offers four main types of scans: Heart Scans (Coronary Artery Calcium Scoring), Lung Scans (for evaluating lung health and detecting issues), Comprehensive Body Scans (for detailed internal structure assessment), and Virtual Colonoscopy (a less invasive colorectal cancer screening option).

Q2: Are these scans safe? What about radiation exposure?

A2: Yes, the scans we offer are generally safe. We use advanced technology calibrated to deliver the lowest possible radiation dose while ensuring diagnostic quality images, strictly adhering to safety guidelines. Our team will discuss any specific concerns related to radiation with you before your procedure.

Q3: How long does a typical scan take at the Bellaire location?

A3: Scan times vary depending on the specific type of examination. A Heart Scan or Lung Scan typically takes about 10-15 minutes. A Comprehensive Body Scan might take 15-30 minutes, and a Virtual Colonoscopy procedure itself is quick, but preparation time is significant. Our staff will provide you with detailed timing information when you schedule your appointment.

Q4: Do I need a referral to get a scan done at Advanced Body Scan Bellaire?

A4: While walk-in appointments are sometimes accommodated, we strongly recommend calling our Bellaire location at (346) 955-7226 or visiting our website. For Heart Scans and Virtual Colonoscopy, a physician referral is typically required. For Lung Scans and Body Scans, an ordering provider is often preferred but not always mandatory; our team can clarify this requirement when you inquire.

Q5: What should I expect during a Heart Scan or Lung Scan at the Bellaire facility?

A5: You can expect a comfortable, non-invasive procedure. You'll change into a provided gown and lie on an examination table. A trained technologist will gently position you and operate the scanner. You may need to hold your breath briefly during image acquisition. The technologist will explain each step before it happens. After the scan, results are typically available within 24-48 hours and will be sent securely to your referring physician.
