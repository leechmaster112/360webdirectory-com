What is Pet Euthanasia?

Pet euthanasia is a humane method of ending an animal's life when continued suffering is inevitable or the quality of life is severely diminished due to illness or injury. It is a carefully administered medication given by a trained professional, similar to how humans receive palliative care at the end of life. The goal is to minimize pain and anxiety, allowing the pet to pass gently and peacefully. This is not a quick or distressing procedure; it is designed to be as comfortable as possible for your beloved companion.
