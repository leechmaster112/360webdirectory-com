Otto Armleder Dog Park

Otto Armleder Dog Park is a 10-acre off-leash dog park on Cincinnati's east side along the Little Miami River. It offers separate areas for large and small breeds, a canine shower, dog drinking fountains, shade trees, and benches for owners, with room for real running. Hours, photos, and directions are on the park's Doggie Park Near Me listing.
