What is a Sober House?

A sober house, like the one you'll find at 1501 S Newkirk St, offers a drug-free, alcohol-free living environment. It serves as a crucial transitional step for men who have completed formal treatment programs or are seeking a stable foundation before entering a more structured recovery program. Our house provides a safe, supportive home where residents can rebuild their daily routines, practice healthy coping mechanisms, and develop a strong support network.

Our Supportive Environment

Living at 1501 S Newkirk St means being surrounded by peers who understand the challenges of recovery. We cultivate an atmosphere of accountability and encouragement. Our house promotes personal growth, sobriety maintenance, and the development of a fulfilling life free from substance dependence.
