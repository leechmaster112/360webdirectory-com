Imran Qureshi, DO: Expert Non-Surgical Interventional Spine Care in Katy, TX

At 23501 Cinco Ranch Blvd G205, Katy, TX 77494, Imran Qureshi, DO, offers specialized, non-surgical interventional care for a wide range of spine-related conditions. Dr. Qureshi is a board-certified physician dedicated to providing compassionate and effective treatment options for individuals experiencing back pain, neck pain, and other spinal discomforts. Our focus is on restoring function, reducing pain, and improving the quality of life for our patients right here in Katy, TX.

Understanding the debilitating nature of spine issues, Dr. Qureshi employs a patient-centered approach. This means that every treatment plan is tailored to the individual's unique needs, diagnosis, and lifestyle. We believe in a thorough evaluation process, which includes a detailed medical history, a comprehensive physical examination, and the utilization of advanced diagnostic imaging when necessary. This meticulous approach ensures that we accurately identify the source of your pain and develop the most appropriate and effective treatment strategy.

Our expertise lies in interventional pain management, a field that utilizes minimally invasive techniques to diagnose and treat pain. These procedures are designed to provide targeted relief, often avoiding the need for more invasive surgical interventions. Dr. Qureshi is highly skilled in a variety of these advanced procedures, including but not limited to:

Epidural Steroid Injections: These injections deliver anti-inflammatory medication directly to the affected area around the spinal nerves, helping to reduce inflammation and alleviate pain. Facet Joint Injections: Targeting the small joints in the spine that can become inflamed and painful, these injections can provide significant relief from stiffness and aching. Medial Branch Blocks: Used to diagnose and treat pain originating from the facet joints, these blocks temporarily numb the nerves that transmit pain signals from these joints. Radiofrequency Ablation (RFA): This procedure uses heat generated by radiofrequency waves to disrupt the nerve pathways that are sending pain signals to the brain. It can provide longer-lasting pain relief for conditions like chronic back pain and facet joint pain. Sacroiliac (SI) Joint Injections: The SI joint can be a source of lower back and hip pain. Injections into this joint can help diagnose the cause of pain and provide relief. Spinal Cord Stimulation: For chronic pain that has not responded to other treatments, spinal cord stimulation involves implanting a device that sends mild electrical impulses to the spinal cord to mask pain signals.

These interventional techniques are performed with precision and care at our Katy, TX facility. Dr. Qureshi's commitment to staying at the forefront of medical advancements ensures that our patients benefit from the latest and most effective treatment modalities available for spine conditions. We aim to provide relief that allows our patients to return to their daily activities with greater comfort and mobility.
