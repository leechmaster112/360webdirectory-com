2. Do you measure windows in Lubbock?

Yes, we do measure windows in Lubbock! You can provide your measurements using our online guide, or you can contact us at 8779311177 to schedule a convenient time for one of our representatives to measure your windows at your Lubbock home or business.

3. How do I measure a window for shades or blinds?

For inside mount shades, measure the width from side to side and the height from top to bottom. For outside mount, measure the desired width and height area where you want the shade to sit. We provide detailed measurement instructions on our website and can guide you over the phone.

4. Do you offer a warranty on your products?

Yes, we stand behind the quality of our American-made products. We offer warranties on our window coverings, typically covering defects in materials and workmanship. The specific warranty details may vary depending on the product and fabric chosen, so please inquire when placing your order.
