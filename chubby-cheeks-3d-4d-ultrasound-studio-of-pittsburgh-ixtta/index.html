Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is the difference between a standard ultrasound and a 3D/4D ultrasound?

A1: Standard ultrasounds are primarily used for medical evaluation during pregnancy. Chubby Cheeks' 3D/4D Ultrasound is an elective service that provides detailed, lifelike images and videos of your baby's face and body, enhancing the bonding experience for parents at our Pittsburgh location.

Q2: Is the 3D/4D ultrasound safe for my baby?

A2: Yes. The transvaginal or abdominal ultrasound gel used at our Chubby Cheeks Pittsburgh studio transmits sound waves, not radiation, making it safe for both mother and baby throughout pregnancy. The technology used is FDA-approved and widely recognized as safe.

Q3: How early can I get a 3D/4D ultrasound at Chubby Cheeks in Pittsburgh?

A3: Detailed 3D/4D images are typically best seen between 14 and 30 weeks of pregnancy. We recommend scheduling your appointment at our Pittsburgh studio around weeks 26-28 for optimal results. Please call our Pittsburgh location at (412) 444-8742 for specific guidance.

Q4: Do I need a prescription for a 3D/4D ultrasound at Chubby Cheeks?

A4: While a 3D/4D ultrasound is considered an elective service and does not require a prescription, we welcome all patients. Our Pittsburgh studio provides a wonderful opportunity for parents-to-be regardless of their pregnancy stage.

Q5: How long does a typical 3D/4D ultrasound appointment take at Chubby Cheeks?

A5: Appointments at our Chubby Cheeks Pittsburgh studio typically last between 45 minutes to an hour. This allows ample time for the technologist to capture the best possible images of your baby and for you to ask any questions you might have.
