Expert Speech and Language Therapy for Children in Orpington

Welcome to Articulate Kids Speech and Language Therapy, your dedicated partner for expert assessment and therapy services located right here in Petts Wood, Orpington, BR5 1NU. We understand that navigating the complexities of speech and language development can be a significant concern for parents and caregivers. Our Orpington clinic is staffed by highly qualified and experienced Speech and Language Therapists who are committed to providing personalised, effective support for children facing challenges in this crucial area.

Our Assessment Process

Our journey begins with a thorough assessment tailored specifically to your child's needs. We begin with an initial consultation where we listen to your concerns and gather background information. This is followed by a formal assessment, which may involve observations, standardised tests, and interaction tasks designed to evaluate your child's communication skills accurately. Our assessments are conducted in a friendly, supportive environment at our Petts Wood clinic, ensuring your child feels comfortable and engaged throughout the process.

Comprehensive Therapy Services

Based on the assessment findings, our therapists develop an individualised treatment plan focused on achieving meaningful goals for your child. Our therapy services at our Orpington location address a wide range of needs, including:

• Articulation and Phonology: Helping children produce speech sounds accurately, targeting specific sound errors.

• Language Development: Addressing delays or difficulties in understanding spoken and written language, or expressing thoughts and ideas verbally or in writing.

• Fluency: Providing support and strategies for children experiencing stuttering or disfluencies.

• Social Communication: Developing skills needed for effective interaction and understanding social cues.

• Stammering: Offering compassionate support and evidence-based techniques tailored to the individual's needs.

All therapy sessions are conducted at our Petts Wood centre, allowing for a focused and consistent therapeutic environment. Our therapists work collaboratively with parents/caregivers, providing practical strategies and activities to support progress both during sessions and at home.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Q: Do you offer services outside of Petts Wood, Orpington?

A: Articulate Kids Speech and Language Therapy currently operates solely from our Petts Wood, Orpington (BR5 1NU) location. Our services are specifically designed for clients residing locally in Orpington.

2. Q: How do I book an appointment at your Petts Wood clinic?

A: You can book an appointment by calling our phone number, +44 20 3858 0765, or by contacting us through our website, https://articulatekids.co.uk/. We will schedule a convenient time for your visit to our Petts Wood clinic.

3. Q: What types of speech and language difficulties do you treat?

A: Our therapists at the Petts Wood clinic specialise in a range of difficulties, including articulation disorders, phonological disorders, language delays, expressive and receptive language difficulties, social communication challenges, and fluency/stammering issues. We conduct assessments and provide therapy based on individual needs.

4. Q: How long does a typical assessment take?

A: The duration of an assessment can vary depending on the child's age, attention span, and the complexity of the case. Generally, the initial assessment process involves an initial consultation and a formal assessment session, which might be spread over one or two visits at our Petts Wood clinic. Our therapists will provide you with a clear timeline after the initial contact.

5. Q: Can I observe a therapy session?

A: While we understand the desire to see therapy in action, confidentiality for the child and the therapeutic process means we are unable to offer parent observation during therapy sessions at our Petts Wood clinic. However, therapists are always happy to discuss the therapy plan and provide information about the techniques being used.
