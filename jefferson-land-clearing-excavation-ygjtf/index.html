Jefferson Land Clearing & Excavation

Based in Jefferson City, TN, Jefferson Land Clearing & Excavation has established itself as a trusted local expert for a comprehensive range of land clearing and excavation services. This family-owned and operated business, led by the husband-and-wife team of Steven and Kayla Dinkler, has become a go-to resource for homeowners, builders, and businesses throughout Jefferson, Hamblen, and Grainger counties. With a strong commitment to their community and a reputation for transparent, high-quality work, the company is playing a pivotal role in the region's ongoing development. The company's services are extensive, covering every phase of groundwork. In the realm of land clearing, they transform overgrown and unusable land into buildable sites. Their offerings include land and lot clearing, site preparation, forestry mulching, and the removal of trees, brush, and stumps. They cater to both residential and commercial clients, undertaking projects of all sizes, from clearing a single lot for a new home to reclaiming old pastureland. In addition to land clearing, Jefferson Land Clearing & Excavation provides a full suite of excavation services. This includes digging for basements and foundations, constructing durable gravel driveways and pads, trenching for utilities and septic systems, and expert land grading to ensure a stable and level base with proper drainage. Their expertise in earthmoving, cut and fill services, and the installation of French drains demonstrates their comprehensive approach to site preparation. The Dinklers, originally from West Virginia, have embraced East Tennessee as their home for the past nine years. Their business philosophy is rooted in the values of honesty and doing the job right the first time. This commitment to their clients is reflected in their transparent pricing and clear communication throughout every project. As a fully licensed and insured company, they provide clients with the assurance of professional and reliable service. The company's deep understanding of East Tennessee's terrain, combined with their investment in powerful, modern equipment, allows them to tackle a wide array of challenges. Their service area extends from their home base in Jefferson City to surrounding communities, including New Market, Strawberry Plains, Dandridge, and Morristown, among others. Jefferson Land Clearing & Excavation's emergence comes at a time of significant growth in Jefferson County. The region is experiencing an influx of new residents and a boom in both residential and commercial construction. This growth translates to a heightened demand for the foundational services that companies like Jefferson Land Clearing & Excavation provide. As more land is developed for housing and new businesses, the need for professional land clearing and site preparation becomes increasingly critical. The company is well-positioned to meet this demand and contribute to the responsible development of the communities they serve.
