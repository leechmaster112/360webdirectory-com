A2: The number of sessions required varies depending on individual factors like hair type, skin tone, and the area being treated. Typically, most clients need a series of 6-8 sessions spaced several weeks apart to achieve optimal and long-lasting results at our Laser Bar.

A3: Treatment times vary based on the size and number of areas being treated. Smaller areas like the upper lip might take only a few minutes, while larger areas like the back or legs could require up to an hour or more at our Barrie clinic.
