Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of photography services do you offer at your London studio?

We offer studio space rentals for various photography needs including portrait sessions (corporate, fashion, family), product photography, event photography setup, and creative photo shoots. We provide the space and basic lighting equipment.

Q2: Can I book the entire studio for a single photoshoot?

Absolutely! We offer full studio rentals for private bookings, ensuring you have complete access to the space and our equipment for your entire project.

Q3: Do you provide props or backdrops for photoshoots?

Yes, we have a selection of basic backdrops and props available for use during your rental. Specific items may be subject to availability and booking details.

Q4: Is the studio suitable for video production besides photography?

Definitely! Our controlled lighting and quiet environment make SoHo Haus ideal for filming short videos, commercials, tutorials, and other content creation projects.

Q5: How far in advance should I book my photoshoot or video session?

We recommend booking as early as you can for popular dates. Standard rentals require booking at least 1-2 weeks in advance, but we can accommodate last-minute requests depending on availability.
