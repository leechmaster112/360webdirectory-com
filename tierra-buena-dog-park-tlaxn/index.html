Tierra Buena Dog Park

Tierra Buena Dog Park is a 3-acre fully fenced off-leash facility on Pebble Hills Blvd in east El Paso, with separate areas for small and large dogs, open space for running, and simple obstacle structures. Pet waste stations, drinking fountains, and benches round out the amenities. Hours, photos, and directions are on the park's Doggie Park Near Me listing.
