Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What medical conditions qualify for medical cannabis in Pennsylvania?

A: In Pennsylvania, qualifying conditions include cancer, HIV/AIDS, epilepsy, multiple sclerosis, chronic pain, PTSD, and other debilitating conditions as determined by the state's medical cannabis program.

Q2: How do I get a medical cannabis card for Pennsylvania?

A: To obtain a Pennsylvania medical cannabis card, you need a physician certification stating you have a qualifying condition. You can then apply online through the Pennsylvania Department of Health's portal.

Q3: What forms of payment do you accept at Ethos Wilkes-Barre?

Q4: Can I purchase edibles at Ethos Wilkes-Barre?

Q5: Are there any restrictions on how much medical cannabis I can purchase at one time?

A: Yes, Pennsylvania law sets limits on the amount of cannabis and specific products you can purchase per 30-day certification period. Our staff will guide you through these regulations.
