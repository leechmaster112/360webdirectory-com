Q4: Can I get X-rays done at your Seattle clinic?

A4: Yes, we offer diagnostic X-rays at our 2324 California Ave SW location when deemed necessary for diagnosis or treatment planning, particularly for chiropractic evaluations. Our team will determine if imaging is required during your initial assessment.

Q5: What should I bring to my first appointment?

A5: For your first visit to our clinic at 2324 California Ave SW, please bring your insurance information (including card and any referral letters if needed), a list of medications you are currently taking, and your identification.
