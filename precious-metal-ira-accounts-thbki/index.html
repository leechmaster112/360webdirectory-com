Q1: What exactly is a Precious Metal IRA?

A: A Precious Metal IRA is a type of self-directed Individual Retirement Account that allows you to invest your retirement funds in physical precious metals like gold, silver, platinum, or palladium, held in an IRS-approved depository. This offers diversification beyond traditional stocks and bonds.
