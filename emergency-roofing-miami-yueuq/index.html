Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is your typical emergency response time?

We understand the urgency of roofing emergencies. Our standard policy is to dispatch a team to assess your situation within 24-48 hours of receiving your emergency call. In cases of significant damage or immediate safety concerns, we often prioritize your request and can be on-site much sooner.

2. Do you service areas outside of Miami and New York?

3. How do you handle insurance claims for roof damage?

Dealing with insurance claims after a roofing emergency can be stressful. We assist you by providing detailed assessments and documentation to support your claim. Our team works with you to navigate the process, ensuring accurate evaluations of the damage and facilitating a smoother claim settlement. We act as a liaison between you and your insurance provider.

4. Can I schedule a routine roof inspection or repair outside of emergency hours?

Absolutely. While we are known for our emergency services, Emergency Roofing Miami also offers regular business hours for preventative inspections and non-emergency repairs. We recommend scheduling these services in advance to ensure availability. Please call our office at 305-985-6360 during business hours to book an appointment.

5. What is the difference between roof repair and replacement, and how do I know which I need?

Roof repair addresses specific issues like damaged shingles, localized leaks, or minor structural problems. It's a targeted fix to restore functionality and protect against minor damage.

Roof replacement involves removing the existing roof and installing a new one, typically necessary when the roof is extensively damaged, outdated, or nearing the end of its lifespan.

We begin with a thorough inspection to determine the extent of the damage. If the damage is widespread or the roof is old, we will recommend replacement for long-term solutions. For smaller, isolated problems, a repair might suffice. We provide a clear assessment and advise you on the best course of action.
