Why is professional installation important for window tinting?

Professional installation is crucial to avoid common issues like bubbling, peeling, and poor visibility. Our experienced technicians at Z Tech Window Tinting have the skills and tools to ensure a clean, seamless application that complies with local regulations and looks factory-installed.

How do I care for my newly tinted windows?

After we complete your service at our Countryside shop, we recommend not rolling down your windows for at least 48-72 hours to allow the film to fully cure and adhere to the glass. After that, you can clean them with a soft, non-abrasive cloth and ammonia-free cleaner to maintain their clarity and finish.
