FAQ

1. What types of window tinting services do you offer at your Countryside location?

At our facility at 9825 W 55th St #D, we specialize in professional automotive window tinting. Our services include the installation of high-performance ceramic, carbon, and dyed window films for side windows, rear windows, and sunroofs. We focus solely on providing top-tier tinting solutions to enhance your vehicle's look, comfort, and protection.

2. How long does a typical window tint installation take at your shop?

The duration for a complete window tint installation can vary depending on the vehicle type and the specific film selected. Most standard vehicles are completed within 2 to 4 hours at our Countryside shop. We ensure each installation is given the time and attention necessary for a perfect, bubble-free finish.

3. Is the window tint you use compliant with Illinois laws?

Yes, absolutely. Our expert team at Z Tech Window Tinting is fully knowledgeable about Illinois window tint regulations. During your consultation at our 9825 W 55th St #D location, we will guide you through the legal tint darkness (VLT%) limits for your specific vehicle to ensure your new tint is fully compliant.

4. How do I care for my newly tinted windows after leaving your facility?

Proper care is essential. We advise all our clients to avoid rolling down their windows and to refrain from cleaning the interior surface of the tint for at least 3-5 days after the installation is completed at our shop. This allows the adhesive to cure completely. After that period, clean with a soft, non-abrasive cloth and an ammonia-free cleaner.
