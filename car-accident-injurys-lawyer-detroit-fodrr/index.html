FAQ

Q: How long do I have to file a claim after my car accident in Detroit? A: In Michigan, the statute of limitations for filing a personal injury claim, including car accidents, is generally 2 years from the date of the accident. It's crucial to consult with us promptly to ensure your rights are protected.

Q: Do I have to pay upfront fees for your services? A: Our fee structure is contingent upon successfully resolving your case. We typically work on a contingency fee basis, meaning you only pay if we recover compensation for you.

Q: How long does it take to resolve a car accident injury case? A: The timeline varies significantly based on factors like the severity of injuries, insurance company cooperation, and potential need for litigation. Many cases settle within a few months, while others may take longer.

Q: What types of injuries do you handle? A: We handle all types of injuries sustained in car accidents, including whiplash, broken bones, spinal injuries, traumatic brain injuries, burns, and injuries leading to long-term disability.

Q: Can you help me with out-of-state drivers or uninsured motorists in Detroit? A: Yes, our firm handles cases involving out-of-state drivers and situations with uninsured or underinsured motorists. We have the expertise to navigate these complexities and ensure you receive fair compensation.
