Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How many laser hair removal sessions are needed?

A: The number of laser hair removal sessions required varies depending on individual factors such as hair type, skin tone, and the area being treated. Typically, most clients require between 6 to 8 sessions spaced several weeks apart to achieve optimal and long-lasting results at our Barrie clinic.

Q2: Is laser hair removal painful?

A: While some clients may experience a mild stinging sensation during the treatment, most find it tolerable. We utilize advanced laser technology with cooling mechanisms to minimize discomfort, and topical numbing creams are available upon request at our 535 Bryne Dr location.

Q3: What is the downtime after microneedling?

A: Microneedling typically involves some redness and mild swelling similar to a sunburn. Most clients experience minimal downtime and can return to their normal activities within a day or two. We recommend avoiding makeup and direct sun exposure for a few days following your treatment at The Laser Bar.

Q4: How soon will I see results from IPL facials?

A: You may notice an immediate improvement in skin tone after an IPL facial, although the most significant results develop gradually over several weeks as new, healthier skin cells replace the older, damaged ones. A series of treatments spaced several weeks apart is usually recommended for optimal outcomes at our Barrie facility.

Q5: Are your lasers and equipment safe?

A: Absolutely. The Laser Bar uses only FDA-approved laser and IPL devices that have been rigorously tested for safety and efficacy. Our equipment is regularly maintained according to manufacturer specifications, and our practitioners are trained professionals who carefully adjust settings based on your skin and hair characteristics during your treatment at 535 Bryne Dr.
