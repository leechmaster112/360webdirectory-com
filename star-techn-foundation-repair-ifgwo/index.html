Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Why would a foundation need repair, especially in the Houston area?

Foundations can be affected by various factors, including soil movement (which can be influenced by heavy rains, drought cycles, or poor drainage common in Houston), inadequate drainage around the home, tree root growth, or simply the natural settling that occurs after construction. These factors can cause cracks, shifting, or bowing in the foundation walls, potentially leading to structural issues.

Q2: What are the signs that my Houston home's foundation might be damaged?

Several signs can indicate foundation problems. These include visible cracks in the foundation walls or floors (especially larger cracks wider than 1/8 inch), doors and windows that stick or no longer close properly, sloping or cracked floors, gaps between walls and floors or ceilings, sticking or unlevel appliances, and cracks in the driveway or sidewalks. Pooling water near the foundation is also a contributing factor.

Q3: How long does a typical foundation repair project take?

The duration of a foundation repair project can vary significantly depending on the type and severity of the problem, the chosen repair method, and access to the affected area. Our team will provide you with a detailed timeline after a thorough inspection of your specific foundation issue at our Houston location.

Q4: Do you offer emergency foundation repair services?

While we strive to accommodate all repair requests promptly, foundation issues that pose an immediate safety risk or allow water intrusion should be addressed as soon as possible. We recommend contacting us immediately if you notice sudden, significant foundation movement or water penetration, as timely intervention is crucial.

Q5: What types of foundation repairs do you perform?

At Star Techn Foundation Repair, we specialize in common foundation repair techniques suitable for Houston homes, including foundation crack repair, underpinning, mud jacking (slab leveling), helical piers, and foundation wall stabilization. Our team will recommend the most appropriate solution based on a comprehensive inspection of your foundation.
