Our Services

We offer comprehensive speech and language therapy tailored to the unique needs of each child. Our assessment process begins with a thorough evaluation to identify specific areas of difficulty, including articulation, fluency, voice, language comprehension and expression, social communication, and literacy development. Based on these findings, we create personalized therapy plans with clear goals and measurable outcomes.

Our therapy sessions are designed to be engaging and effective, incorporating play-based activities, conversation practice, and specialized techniques to help children develop essential communication skills. We work closely with parents and caregivers to provide strategies that can be practiced at home, ensuring consistent progress between sessions.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What ages of children do you work with?

We provide services for children from birth up to young adulthood, addressing speech, language, and communication needs at any age.

2. How long does therapy typically last?

The duration of therapy varies depending on the child's needs, goals, and progress. Most children show significant improvement within a few months, but we tailor our approach to ensure lasting results.

3. Do you work with schools in Petts Wood?

Yes, we collaborate with local schools in Petts Wood to provide in-school assessments and therapy sessions when appropriate.

4. What conditions do you treat?

We address a range of speech, language, and communication difficulties including articulation disorders, language delays, stuttering, social communication challenges, and literacy issues.

5. Can I accompany my child to appointments?

Absolutely! Parents and caregivers are welcome to attend assessment sessions and are provided with practical strategies to support communication at home.
