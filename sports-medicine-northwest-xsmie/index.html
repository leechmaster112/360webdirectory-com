Q1: What conditions do you treat at your Seattle location?

This includes back and neck pain, sports injuries, joint pain, headaches originating from the neck, sciatica, carpal tunnel syndrome, tendonitis, and repetitive strain injuries. We also focus on helping athletes optimize performance and prevent injuries.

Q2: Do you accept insurance?

We are committed to working with a variety of insurance plans. Please visit our website or call our Seattle office at (206) 682-0676 to confirm your specific insurance coverage and understand our participation details.

Q3: How long does a typical chiropractic adjustment take?

The duration of your visit depends on your specific condition and treatment plan. Your initial visit usually takes about 60-90 minutes and includes a thorough consultation, examination, and the first adjustment. Follow-up visits typically range from 30 to 45 minutes.

Q4: What should I expect during my first appointment?

Your first appointment at Sports Medicine Northwest in Seattle involves a comprehensive health history intake, followed by a physical examination. We will discuss your concerns, perform movement analysis, and neurological and orthopedic tests if needed. Based on our findings, we will develop a personalized treatment plan and may perform your first adjustment during this visit.

Q5: Can I schedule same-day appointments?

While we strive to accommodate all patients, same-day appointments are sometimes available, especially for urgent care needs. Calling our Seattle office directly at (206) 682-0676 is the best way to inquire about scheduling options.
