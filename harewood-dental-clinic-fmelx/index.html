Our Comprehensive Range of Dental Services

We offer a wide array of dental services designed to address all aspects of oral health. Whether you are seeking preventative care, restorative treatment, or cosmetic enhancement, our Nanaimo clinic is equipped to meet your needs.

Dental Check-Ups and Cleanings

Regular dental examinations and professional cleanings are the cornerstone of good oral hygiene. Our skilled dental hygienists and dentists conduct thorough examinations to detect any potential issues early, while our cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup, helping to prevent gum disease and maintain fresh breath.

Filling Cavities

If you experience tooth decay, our Nanaimo clinic offers effective cavity filling services. Using modern materials, we can restore your tooth's function and appearance, relieving discomfort and preventing further damage.

Root Canal Therapy

When the inner pulp of a tooth becomes infected or inflamed, root canal therapy may be necessary to save the tooth. Our experienced endodontists in Nanaimo perform this procedure with precision and care, utilizing advanced technology to minimize discomfort and ensure a successful outcome.

Dental Crowns

Dental crowns are custom-made coverings that restore damaged or weakened teeth. Whether your tooth has been compromised by decay, injury, or a large filling, a crown can provide strength, stability, and a natural appearance, all tailored to fit perfectly at our Nanaimo practice.

Dental Bridges

For missing teeth, dental bridges offer a fixed solution. These prosthetic teeth are anchored to adjacent teeth or dental implants, filling gaps and restoring your ability to chew and speak comfortably. We provide this service exclusively from our Nanaimo location.

Dental Implants

Missing multiple teeth? Dental implants provide a durable, natural-looking solution. We offer implant services at our Nanaimo clinic, including the placement of titanium posts that fuse with your jawbone and support custom-crafted crowns, bridges, or dentures.
