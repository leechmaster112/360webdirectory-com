Our Comprehensive Services

We offer a wide range of dental services designed to address all your oral health needs. Whether you require preventative care, restorative treatment, or cosmetic enhancement, our team at Harewood Dental Clinic is here to help you achieve a healthy, beautiful smile.

Dental Checkups and Cleanings

Regular dental checkups and professional cleanings are essential for maintaining good oral health. At Harewood Dental Clinic, our skilled dental hygienists perform thorough cleanings to remove plaque and tartar buildup, while our dentists conduct comprehensive examinations to detect any potential issues early on. We recommend bi-annual visits for most patients to ensure optimal oral health.

Fillings and Restorations

If you experience tooth decay or damage, our Nanaimo clinic offers effective filling solutions. We utilize modern dental materials and techniques to restore your teeth to their full function and appearance. Our goal is to provide durable, comfortable restorations that help you maintain your natural smile.

Crowns and Bridges

For more extensive restorations, we offer dental crowns and bridges. Crowns are custom-made coverings that protect damaged teeth and improve their appearance, while bridges can replace missing teeth by anchoring to adjacent teeth or dental implants. These restorative options help restore your ability to eat comfortably and speak clearly.

Root Canal Therapy

When a tooth's inner pulp becomes infected or inflamed, root canal therapy may be necessary to save the tooth. Our experienced endodontists perform this procedure with precision and care, utilizing advanced technology to minimize discomfort and ensure successful outcomes.

Dental Implants

Missing teeth can affect both your appearance and oral function. At Harewood Dental Clinic, we offer dental implants as a durable and natural-looking solution for tooth replacement. These titanium posts integrate with your jawbone, providing a stable foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures.

Oral Surgery

Our clinic provides various oral surgery services, including tooth extractions (both simple and surgical), gum surgery, and bone grafting. Our surgical procedures are performed with the highest level of care and precision to ensure patient safety and comfort.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Beyond restorative care, we also offer cosmetic dental services to enhance your smile's appearance. Procedures like teeth whitening, veneers, and dental bonding can address issues such as discoloration, chips, gaps, and misalignment, helping you achieve the confident smile you desire.

Emergency Dental Care

Dental emergencies can happen unexpectedly. At Harewood Dental Clinic, we understand the importance of prompt treatment in these situations. While we recommend scheduling appointments for non-urgent matters, we are committed to providing necessary emergency care to alleviate pain and address urgent issues.
