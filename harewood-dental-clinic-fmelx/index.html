Our Services Include:

Dental Check-ups and Cleanings: Regular dental examinations and professional cleanings are the foundation of good oral health. Our team performs thorough oral assessments, including checking for cavities, gum disease, and oral cancer during your visit. We also provide personalized advice on maintaining good oral hygiene at home.

Filling Cavities: We effectively treat dental cavities (dental caries) using modern composite fillings. These materials bond well with tooth structure, providing durable and aesthetically pleasing restorations.

Root Canal Therapy: When the inner pulp of a tooth becomes infected or inflamed, root canal therapy is often necessary to save the tooth. Our clinic offers this specialized treatment using advanced techniques and equipment to ensure patient comfort and successful outcomes.

Teeth Whitening: We offer professional teeth whitening services to help our Nanaimo patients achieve a brighter, more confident smile. Using safe and effective procedures, we can significantly enhance the colour of your natural teeth.

Crowns and Bridges: For damaged or missing teeth, we provide durable and natural-looking restorations. Dental crowns (caps) can restore a damaged tooth, while dental bridges can replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring to adjacent healthy teeth.

Dental Implants: We offer dental implant services to replace missing teeth. Dental implants are a long-lasting solution that replaces both the root and the visible portion of the tooth, providing a stable and natural-looking restoration.

Wisdom Teeth Removal: Wisdom teeth removal is a common procedure we perform for patients in Nanaimo. Our team ensures a comfortable and efficient extraction process.
