Location

Fukui Sushi, located at 1612 Bayview Ave, Toronto, ON M4G 3B7, invites you to experience the art of authentic Japanese sushi and cuisine. Our establishment is dedicated to bringing you the finest selection of sushi, sashimi, and traditional Japanese dishes prepared with meticulous care and the freshest ingredients.

At Fukui Sushi, we pride ourselves on authenticity. Our chefs, trained in the rich culinary traditions of Japan, create each piece of sushi by hand, ensuring a perfect balance of flavor, texture, and presentation. Whether you are a sushi connoisseur or a first-time visitor, our menu offers a delightful journey through the world of Japanese gastronomy.

For those seeking an unforgettable dining experience, we offer the Omakase service. This unique culinary adventure allows our expert chefs to select the freshest ingredients and prepare a personalized course of sushi and other Japanese delicacies tailored to your preferences. The Omakase experience at Fukui Sushi is a testament to our commitment to quality and innovation.

Beyond sushi, our menu features a variety of other Japanese dishes, including hot pots, tempura, and rice bowls, all prepared using time-honored recipes and techniques. We believe that every dish tells a story, and at Fukui Sushi, every bite is a celebration of tradition and flavor.

Visit us at 1612 Bayview Ave, Toronto, ON M4G 3B7 to discover why Fukui Sushi is a beloved destination for sushi lovers in this neighborhood. We look forward to welcoming you and sharing the authentic taste of Japan with you.

For more information or to make a reservation, please visit our website at https://www.fukuisushitoronto.com/ or call us at (416) 487-3388.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What makes Fukui Sushi's sushi special?

A: Fukui Sushi's sushi is crafted using the freshest ingredients and traditional Japanese techniques. Our chefs focus on authenticity, ensuring each piece is prepared with care and precision.

Q: What are Fukui Sushi's business hours?

A: Fukui Sushi is open for lunch and dinner from Monday to Sunday, typically from 11:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Please check our website for the most up-to-date hours.

Q: Do I need to make a reservation?

A: While walk-ins are welcome, we recommend making a reservation, especially for the Omakase experience or during peak hours, to ensure a table is available for you at 1612 Bayview Ave.

Q: What is Omakase at Fukui Sushi?

A: Omakase is a premium dining experience where our chef selects and prepares a course of sushi and other Japanese dishes based on the day's freshest ingredients. It's an opportunity to enjoy a curated selection of culinary delights.

Q: Do you offer catering services?

A: Yes, Fukui Sushi offers catering services for private events. Please contact us at (416) 487-3388 for more details and to discuss your catering needs.
