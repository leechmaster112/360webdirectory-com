Reyna Pilates

Reyna Pilates is a boutique mat and reformer Pilates studio in Downtown Odessa, Texas, offering intentional, beginner-friendly movement in an elevated and welcoming environment. Classes are designed to build full-body strength, improve mobility, support balance, posture and core control, and help clients move with greater confidence. Reyna Pilates offers small-group reformer Pilates, mat Pilates, heated Pilates classes, introductory sessions and community Pilates events for a range of fitness levels. Whether you are new to Pilates or looking to deepen your practice, the studio provides thoughtful instruction focused on alignment, breath, control and sustainable progress.

Q: What kind of results can I expect from consistent Pilates practice at Reyna Pilates?
