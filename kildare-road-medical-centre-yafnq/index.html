Location

Kildare Road Medical Centre, located at 36 Kildare Road, Blacktown, NSW, 2148, has been a cornerstone of primary healthcare in the Blacktown community since its doors opened in April 2010. Following a comprehensive refurbishment of the historic building, the centre was established with a commitment to providing high-quality, patient-focused medical services within a comfortable and modern environment.

At Kildare Road Medical Centre, our dedicated team of experienced medical practitioners is dedicated to the health and well-being of our patients residing in the Blacktown area. We pride ourselves on offering a comprehensive range of general medical services, delivered with the utmost professionalism and compassion. Our focus is on building lasting relationships with our patients, ensuring they receive consistent, high-quality care close to home.

Our general practitioners (GPs) are skilled in diagnosing and managing a wide array of common and complex health issues. We provide thorough health assessments, manage chronic diseases such as asthma, diabetes, hypertension, and cardiovascular conditions, and offer evidence-based treatments for acute illnesses like respiratory infections, urinary tract infections, and minor injuries.

We understand the importance of preventative healthcare and encourage regular check-ups. Our services include comprehensive health screenings, tailored immunisation programs, and lifestyle advice aimed at promoting long-term wellness and preventing illness. We are committed to staying abreast of the latest medical research and guidelines to ensure our patients receive the most current and effective care.

For patients requiring further investigation or specialist opinion, we facilitate appropriate referrals to diagnostic services and specialist consultants, ensuring a smooth and coordinated pathway to comprehensive care. Our centre is equipped to handle minor procedures and wound care, providing essential medical attention for a variety of conditions.

We strive to create a welcoming and accessible environment for all patients. Our friendly administrative staff is always on hand to assist with appointments, inquiries, and general practice management. We believe that clear communication and patient education are fundamental to effective healthcare.

Whether you require a routine health check, management of an ongoing condition, or treatment for an acute illness, Kildare Road Medical Centre is your dedicated healthcare partner in Blacktown. We are committed to providing expert medical care, personalised attention, and a supportive environment for you and your family.

Experience the difference of dedicated healthcare delivered locally. Visit us at 36 Kildare Road, Blacktown, or contact our friendly team at (02) 8822 3000 or visit our website at https://www.kildaremedical.com.au/.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of general medical services do you offer at Kildare Road Medical Centre?

Our centre provides a comprehensive range of general medical services, including diagnosis and treatment of common illnesses (like colds, flu, and infections), management of chronic conditions (such as diabetes and hypertension), health screenings, minor procedures, wound care, and preventative health advice tailored to individual needs.

Q2: Can I get referrals to specialists from Kildare Road Medical Centre?

Yes, our experienced GPs can assess your condition and provide appropriate referrals to specialist consultants when necessary. We work to ensure a smooth and coordinated transition to ensure you receive the best possible care.

Q3: Do you offer immunisation services for adults and children?

Absolutely. We offer a range of immunisation services for patients of all ages, including influenza vaccines and recommended adult and childhood vaccinations. Our team can discuss your specific needs and provide advice on immunisation schedules.

Q4: How do I book an appointment at Kildare Road Medical Centre?

Appointments can be booked easily by calling our friendly reception team at (02) 8822 3000. You can also find contact information and potentially online booking options on our website at https://www.kildaremedical.com.au/.

Q5: What is the process for managing chronic diseases like diabetes at your centre?

We offer comprehensive management for chronic diseases. This involves regular health assessments, monitoring relevant health indicators, discussing treatment plans, providing lifestyle modification advice, and coordinating care with other health professionals as needed. Our goal is to help you effectively manage your condition and maintain your quality of life.
