FAQ

Q: What is Scalp Micropigmentation (SMP)?

A: Scalp Micropigmentation is a non-surgical cosmetic procedure that involves depositing pigment onto the scalp to simulate the look of a shaved head or a natural hairline, effectively concealing hair loss.

Q: How long does an SMP session take?

A: The duration of an SMP session varies based on the treatment area and desired density. Initial consultations are shorter, while full treatment packages typically require multiple sessions spread over several weeks.

Q: Is SMP painful?

A: Most clients experience minimal discomfort during the procedure. We utilize topical anesthetics to ensure a comfortable experience throughout your treatment at our Stockton clinic.

Q: How long do SMP results last?

A: SMP results are considered permanent, although they may gradually fade over time due to sun exposure and skin regeneration. Top-up sessions are usually recommended every 1-3 years to maintain optimal pigment density.

Q: Can SMP help with a receding hairline?

A: Yes, one of the primary benefits of Scalp Micropigmentation is its ability to camouflage a receding hairline, creating a denser, more youthful appearance without surgery.
