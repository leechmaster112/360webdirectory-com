Frequently Asked Questions

What types of anxiety do you treat?

We treat various anxiety disorders, including Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD), Social Anxiety Disorder, Panic Disorder, Agoraphobia, and Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD). Our therapists are trained to understand the nuances of each condition and tailor treatment accordingly.

How does trauma therapy work?

Trauma therapy at Optimum Joy Counseling often involves evidence-based approaches like EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) or CPT (Cognitive Processing Therapy). We work with you to process traumatic memories, challenge unhelpful beliefs formed from the experience, manage associated symptoms like flashbacks or hypervigilance, and develop skills for emotional regulation and safety.

What should I expect in my first session?

Your first session is typically an intake or consultation. You'll have the opportunity to share your background, reasons for seeking therapy, and your goals for treatment. Your therapist will gather information to understand your situation better and explain their approach. This session helps establish the therapeutic relationship and sets the foundation for your journey.

How often are therapy sessions?

We typically offer weekly sessions to allow for consistent progress and support. However, we understand individual needs may vary, and we can discuss flexible scheduling options with you.

Do you accept insurance?

Optimum Joy Counseling operates as an out-of-network provider for many insurance plans. We recommend verifying your insurance benefits, including deductible, co-pay, and out-of-network coverage limits, before starting therapy. We can provide necessary documentation for reimbursement if applicable.
