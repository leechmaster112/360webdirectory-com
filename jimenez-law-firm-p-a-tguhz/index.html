Jimenez Law Firm, P.A.

Jimenez Law Firm, P.A. is a distinguished Florida-based legal practice specializing in personal injury and real estate law, with its primary office serving Jacksonville and surrounding communities throughout Northeast Florida. Founded by attorney Tomas Jimenez, a Florida State University graduate with dual Bachelor of Science degrees in Real Estate and Business Management, the firm has established itself as a trusted advocate for injury victims and property transaction clients across the state. The firm's personal injury practice encompasses a comprehensive range of accident and injury cases, including car accidents, trucking accidents, motorcycle collisions, bicycle accidents, pedestrian injuries, slip and fall incidents, medical malpractice claims, and dog bite cases. With a proven track record of substantial settlements ranging from $400,000 to $900,000 in recent cases, Jimenez Law Firm demonstrates consistent success in securing maximum compensation for clients facing medical expenses, lost wages, pain and suffering, and long-term care needs. Operating on a contingency fee basis, the firm ensures accessible legal representation for all clients regardless of their financial situation. The legal team, led by founding partner Tomas Jimenez and associate attorney Kelsey McCusker, brings extensive experience and community involvement to every case. Mr. Jimenez, a graduate of Florida A&M University College of Law with prior experience as a legal intern for the Office of the Attorney General of Florida, combines his business acumen with legal expertise to provide strategic representation. Ms. McCusker, a Florida Coastal School of Law graduate and Pro Bono Honors Award recipient, contributes her dedication to public service and active involvement in the Jacksonville Women's Lawyers Association, Jacksonville Justice Association, and Jacksonville Bar Association Young Lawyers Division.

Q2: How long do I have to file a personal injury claim in Jacksonville, Florida?

A2: In Jacksonville, as in the rest of Florida, the statute of limitations for most personal injury claims is four years from the date of the injury. It is crucial to consult with an attorney at the Jimenez Law Firm, P.A. as soon as possible to ensure your claim is filed within this time frame. Delay can jeopardize your ability to recover compensation.

Q3: Do I need to pay anything upfront to hire Jimenez Law Firm, P.A.?

A3: No, the Jimenez Law Firm, P.A. operates on a contingency fee basis. This means you won't owe any legal fees unless we successfully resolve your case. Our fee is typically a percentage of the settlement or court award you receive. This arrangement allows you to access quality legal representation without any upfront costs.

Q4: What should I do immediately after being injured in Jacksonville?

A4: After being injured in Jacksonville, our advice is to seek prompt medical attention for your injuries, even if they seem minor. Document the scene if it's safe to do so (take photos, gather witness information), and contact the Jimenez Law Firm, P.A. at (904) 559-5600 or visit our website https://www.jimenez-lawfirm.com/ to schedule a consultation. Early action is key to protecting your rights and building a strong case.

Q5: How long does a personal injury case typically take in Jacksonville?

A5: The duration of a personal injury case in Jacksonville varies significantly depending on factors such as the complexity of the case, the severity of the injuries, the willingness of the responsible party to settle, and potential disputes over liability or damages. Simple cases might be resolved in a few months, while more complex cases can take a year or longer. Our team at the Jimenez Law Firm, P.A. will keep you informed about the progress of your case.
