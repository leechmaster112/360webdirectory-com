Q2: How often should I schedule a dental check-up at Chicopee Street Dental?

We generally recommend scheduling a routine dental check-up and cleaning every six months. Regular visits help maintain optimal oral health, prevent issues like cavities and gum disease, and allow our team to address any concerns promptly. However, the frequency may vary based on individual needs, which our dentist can determine during your examination.

While we strive to accommodate all patients, our practice primarily focuses on scheduled appointments. However, in case of a serious dental emergency occurring in the Chicopee area, we will make every effort to assist you, often on the same day, depending on the nature and severity of the issue.
