A1: Our clinic at 3200 Greenfield Road treats a variety of conditions, including varicose veins, spider veins, chronic venous insufficiency (CVI), restless leg syndrome, and other venous disorders that can cause symptoms like pain, swelling, heaviness, or cosmetic concerns.

Q2: Do I need a referral to schedule an appointment at your Dearborn center?

A2: While a referral is not always required, we welcome patients who have questions or concerns. For complex cases or if your insurance requires a referral, we can often accommodate this. You can call our Dearborn location directly at 313-488-2737 to discuss your specific situation.

Q3: What treatment options are available for spider veins and varicose veins in Dearborn?

A3: We offer several advanced, minimally invasive treatments tailored to different vein conditions. A4: Recovery time varies depending on the procedure performed and individual factors. Most patients experience minimal downtime and can return to light activities within a day or two. We provide detailed post-operative instructions at our Dearborn center to ensure a smooth recovery.

Q5: Does your vein treatment center accept insurance for patients in Dearborn?
