Our Services

At Nail Salon Malvern, we offer a comprehensive range of services designed to meet your nail care needs. Our skilled technicians are trained in the latest techniques to ensure you leave looking and feeling your best.

Manicures: We provide a variety of manicure options, from basic nail shaping and cuticle care to luxurious treatments incorporating paraffin dips, hand massages, and nourishing creams. We cater to all preferences, including classic, French, or colorful designs.

Pedicures: Indulge your feet with our professional pedicure services. We offer classic pedicures focusing on nail shaping, cuticle care, and foot soak treatments. For those seeking extra pampering, we also provide deluxe pedicures featuring polish application and meticulous foot care.

Nail Enhancements: Discover the transformative power of nail extensions. We specialize in applying durable and attractive enhancements like acrylic nails, dip powder nails, and soft gel nails. We can also assist with nail art designs and intricate nail decoration to express your unique style.

Manicure & Pedicure Packages: We understand the desire for a complete beauty experience. We offer convenient packages that combine our manicure and pedicure services, allowing you to refresh both hands and feet in one visit for maximum relaxation and efficiency.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What services do you offer at your Malvern location?

A1: We offer a full range of nail services including classic and deluxe manicures, classic and deluxe pedicures, nail enhancements (acrylic, dip powder, soft gel), and manicure/pedicure packages tailored for our Malvern clients.

Q2: How long does a typical manicure or pedicure take?

A2: A standard manicure typically takes around 30-45 minutes, while a deluxe manicure may take 50-60 minutes. Standard pedicures usually take 45-60 minutes, and deluxe pedicures can range from 60-90 minutes depending on the specific treatment.

Q3: Do you offer nail art and designs?

A3: Yes, our skilled technicians at Nail Salon Malvern can create various nail art designs, including French tips, ombre effects, glitter, intricate patterns, and more, based on your preferences.

Q4: What should I do to prepare for my appointment?

A4: For your appointment, please bring your nail polish remover if you prefer a specific shade not shown in our display. It's also helpful to have a list of any specific requests or concerns you have regarding your nail care. We recommend booking your appointment in advance, especially for weekends.

Q5: Do you provide nail care advice and maintenance tips?

A5: Absolutely! Our team is happy to provide personalized advice on nail care and maintenance between appointments. We can offer tips on keeping your nails healthy at home and suggest the best way to maintain your enhancements between salon visits.
