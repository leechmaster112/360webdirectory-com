Law Office of Adrian Chapa III

The Law Office of Adrian Chapa III, PLLC is a full-service law firm located at 1302 Washington St. in Laredo, Texas, serving clients throughout Webb County, Jim Hogg County, Zapata County, and the surrounding South Texas region. Attorney Adrian Chapa III graduated from St. Mary's University School of Law in 2002 and began his career at the Webb County Public Defender's Office. From 2005 to 2015, he served as an Assistant Federal Public Defender in the Laredo Division, gaining a decade of hands-on federal courtroom experience that gives his clients a distinct advantage — especially in federal drug, conspiracy, and firearms cases. The firm handles criminal defense at both the state and federal level, including DWI, drug offenses, felonies, misdemeanors, and expunctions of criminal records. Personal injury services include car accidents, 18-wheeler and commercial truck crashes, motorcycle accidents, slip and fall, and wrongful death. Civil law services include wills, probate, power of attorney, leases, and collections. Adrian Chapa III personally handles every case — no associates, no hand-offs. Bilingual legal services in English and Spanish. Available 24/7. Free initial consultations. Call (956) 723-5520.
