Korl tattoo shop

Korl Tattoo is a tattoo shop in Denver, CO, located in the Art District on Santa Fe. This tattoo shop focuses on custom tattoo work, from bold, colorful pieces to black and grey designs, with an emphasis on strong composition and clean execution. In addition to tattooing, Korl Tattoo offers piercing and permanent make-up services. Clients can request a consultation to discuss ideas, placement, and scope of the project, then book an appointment, with limited walk-in availability when artists are available.
