Location

Pro-Action Health and Performance - Physiotherapy Kanata

Located at 308 Palladium Dr #108, Kanata, ON K2V 1A1, Pro-Action Health and Performance is dedicated to providing expert physiotherapy and comprehensive health and performance services to the residents of Kanata. Our clinic is built on a foundation of trust, advanced clinical skills, and a genuine commitment to helping individuals recover from injuries and enhance their overall well-being.

Our primary focus is on the treatment and management of musculoskeletal injuries, with a strong emphasis on soft tissue issues. This includes common conditions such as strains, sprains, tendonitis, muscle tears, and ligamentous injuries. Our team of dedicated physiotherapists possesses extensive experience in assessing and treating a wide range of soft tissue pathologies, utilizing evidence-based practices to ensure effective and efficient recovery.

We understand that each patient's journey is unique. Therefore, our approach at Pro-Action Health and Performance is highly personalized. We begin with a thorough assessment to accurately diagnose the root cause of your pain or limitation. Based on this assessment, we develop a customized treatment plan tailored specifically to your needs, goals, and capabilities. This plan may incorporate a combination of manual therapy techniques, therapeutic exercises, modalities, and patient education.

Our goal is not just to alleviate pain but also to restore function, prevent future injuries, and empower our patients to manage their condition effectively. We believe in active participation, guiding you through exercises and strategies that you can continue at home to maximize your recovery and long-term health. Whether you're an athlete pushing your limits or someone dealing with everyday wear and tear, our Kanata location is your dedicated partner in regaining your mobility and improving your quality of life.

At Pro-Action Health and Performance, we are committed to providing compassionate, high-quality care within our clinic at 308 Palladium Dr #108, Kanata. We utilize the latest techniques and maintain a focus on patient-centered care to ensure you receive the best possible treatment for your soft tissue injuries and related conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Do you treat soft tissue injuries?

A1: Yes, Pro-Action Health and Performance at 308 Palladium Dr #108, Kanata specializes in the treatment of soft tissue injuries. We commonly address conditions like muscle strains, ligament sprains, tendonitis, and ligament tears.

Q2: What should I bring to my first appointment?

A2: For your initial assessment at our Kanata clinic, please bring your valid health card information, any relevant previous diagnostic reports (X-rays, MRI scans, etc.), a list of current medications, and details of your symptoms. Comfortable clothing is also recommended.

Q3: Are your services covered by insurance?

A3: Pro-Action Health and Performance staff can assist you in understanding your insurance coverage. Many extended health benefit plans cover physiotherapy services. Please bring your insurance information card to your appointment at our Kanata location.

Q4: Do you offer payment plans?

A4: We accept various payment methods including cash, all major credit cards, and direct billing to some extended health benefit plans. Please inquire at our reception desk in Kanata regarding specific payment arrangements.

Q5: How long does a typical treatment plan last?

A5: The duration of treatment varies significantly depending on the nature and severity of your injury, your overall health, and your commitment to the prescribed home exercise program. Our initial assessment helps determine a realistic timeframe, and our team will discuss your progress regularly at our Kanata clinic.
