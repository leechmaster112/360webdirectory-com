Welcome to Lucky Detroit Coffee Roasters Espresso Grosse Pointe, located at 17001 Kercheval Ave, Grosse Pointe, MI 48230. We are proud to serve the Grosse Pointe community from our very own roastery and espresso bar, bringing exceptional coffee directly to you right here in our neighborhood.

At our Grosse Pointe location, we are more than just a coffee shop; we are a dedicated craft roastery and espresso establishment. Our journey began with a passion for the finest coffee beans, and we've meticulously built our process around sourcing exceptional beans and transforming them into truly memorable coffee experiences right here on Kercheval Avenue.

Our espresso program is at the heart of what we do. We meticulously craft our espresso blends, focusing on consistency, flavor complexity, and a perfect crema. Our skilled baristas take pride in pulling exceptional shots, creating velvety microfoam, and expertly crafting a wide range of espresso-based drinks – from perfectly pulled doubles and cappuccinos to Americanos and lattes, all made-to-order with the freshest beans available.

Our commitment extends beyond just espresso. We offer a rotating selection of single-origin pour-overs, showcasing the unique characteristics of beans from different regions. Our knowledgeable team is always ready to guide you towards the perfect pour-over experience, highlighting the subtle nuances and flavors profile of each featured single origin. We also curate a selection of house-made pastries and light snacks, crafted to complement the bold flavors of our coffee.

Step into our cozy and inviting atmosphere at 17001 Kercheval Ave, and you'll find a space designed for both casual coffee breaks and focused work sessions. We pride ourselves on providing a welcoming environment where our regulars feel at home and visitors feel immediately comfortable. Our focus is entirely on delivering a premium coffee experience right here in Grosse Pointe.
