Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of pets do you treat at your Concord location?

A1: We primarily focus on dogs and cats. Occasionally, we may accommodate other small animals based on the specific circumstances and the expertise of our team.

Q2: Do you offer after-hours emergency care for pets?

A2: Yes, our urgent care services are designed to address unexpected health issues outside of regular business hours. Please call our phone number for specific guidance during non-business hours.

Q3: Can I schedule routine wellness exams for my pet?

A3: Absolutely! Regular check-ups are essential for maintaining your pet's health. We offer comprehensive wellness exams to assess your pet's condition and provide appropriate recommendations.

Q4: What should I do in case of a pet emergency?

A4: For true emergencies, please call our clinic immediately. If our facility is closed and you believe your pet is experiencing a life-threatening condition, please seek emergency veterinary care from a 24-hour emergency facility in the Concord area.

Q5: Are payment plans available for necessary pet care?

A5: We strive to make quality veterinary care accessible. We accept various payment methods and can discuss payment options for necessary treatments. Please contact our office for detailed information on payment arrangements.
