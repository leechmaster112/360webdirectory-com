A sober living house provides a structured, supportive, and drug-free environment for individuals in recovery from substance use disorders. It offers stability and a community focus to help maintain sobriety.

Typically, we accept men who are committed to maintaining sobriety and are either transitioning out of formal treatment or seeking a stable living situation to support their ongoing recovery efforts in New York.
