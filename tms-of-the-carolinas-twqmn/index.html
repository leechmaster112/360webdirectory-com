Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) about TMS of the Carolinas (Charlotte Location)

Q1: What is NeuroStar TMS therapy?

A: NeuroStar TMS is a non-invasive treatment for depression that uses focused magnetic pulses to stimulate specific areas of the brain associated with mood regulation. Our Charlotte center utilizes this FDA-cleared technology.

Q2: Is TMS therapy at your Charlotte location right for me?

A: TMS therapy is typically recommended for adults experiencing depression symptoms that haven't responded adequately to previous treatments or medication trials. Our Charlotte team can evaluate your specific situation during a consultation.

Q3: How does the TMS treatment process work at your Charlotte facility?

A: The process involves an initial assessment, mapping of the brain, and then sessions where the NeuroStar device delivers magnetic pulses. Each session is comfortable, takes about an hour, and is conducted daily at our Charlotte location.

Q4: Are there any side effects associated with TMS at your Charlotte center?

A: TMS is generally well-tolerated. Potential side effects, like mild headache or scalp discomfort, are usually temporary. Our Charlotte team monitors you closely during treatment to ensure your comfort.

Q5: Does TMS therapy require anesthesia or sedation at your Charlotte location?

A: No, NeuroStar TMS does not require sedation or anesthesia. You remain awake and alert during each treatment session at our Charlotte facility.
