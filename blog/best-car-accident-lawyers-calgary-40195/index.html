Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What should I do immediately after a car accident in Calgary?
After ensuring everyone's safety and calling emergency services if necessary, you should gather information from the other driver, note details of the scene, and seek medical attention. Contacting a lawyer as soon as possible is also highly recommended to protect your rights.
How do I find the best car accident lawyers Calgary offers?
Look for lawyers with specific experience in car accident claims, a strong track record of success, good client reviews, and who offer free initial consultations. Firms like Preszler Injury Lawyers are often a good starting point.
Can I afford a top car accident lawyer in Calgary if I have limited funds?
Many of the best car accident lawyers in Calgary, including Preszler Injury Lawyers, work on a contingency fee basis. This means they only get paid if they win your case, so you won't owe legal fees upfront.
What compensation can I expect from a car accident claim in Calgary?
Compensation can include medical expenses, lost wages, pain and suffering, and future care costs. The amount varies greatly depending on the severity of injuries and the specifics of the accident.
How long does a car accident claim typically take in Calgary?
The timeline can vary. Minor claims might be settled within months, while more complex cases involving severe injuries could take years. A lawyer can give you a more accurate estimate.
Do I need a lawyer for a minor car accident in Calgary?
Even for minor accidents, insurance companies may try to settle for less than you deserve. A lawyer can help ensure you receive fair compensation for all damages, including future implications.
What distinguishes Preszler Injury Lawyers from other car accident lawyers in Calgary?
Preszler Injury Lawyers is known for its dedicated focus on personal injury, extensive experience, aggressive advocacy, client-focused approach, and a strong history of successful outcomes for accident victims in Calgary.
How can I compare different car accident lawyers Calgary has available?
Review their websites for practice areas and testimonials, check their case results, and schedule consultations with a few different firms to find the best fit for your needs and personality.
What is a "no win, no fee" arrangement for car accident lawyers Calgary?
This arrangement means you don't pay attorney fees unless your lawyer successfully recovers compensation for you. It’s a common practice for personal injury lawyers.
Are there specific laws in Calgary that affect car accident claims?
Yes, Alberta has specific legislation governing motor vehicle accidents and insurance. Experienced car accident lawyers Calgary are well-versed in these laws and how they apply to your case.
