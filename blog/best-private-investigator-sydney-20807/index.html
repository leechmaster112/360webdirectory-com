Frequently Asked Questions

  • What does a private investigator in Sydney do?

    A private investigator conducts surveillance, gathers information, and provides evidence for various cases, including infidelity, corporate fraud, and background checks. Their goal is to uncover facts and provide clients with actionable information.

  • How much does a private investigator cost in Sydney?

    The cost of hiring a private investigator in Sydney varies based on the scope, complexity, and duration of the investigation. Rates are typically hourly, with additional costs for surveillance and other expenses.

  • Are private investigators in Sydney licensed?

    Yes, all private investigators operating in Sydney are required to be licensed by the New South Wales government. This licensing ensures they meet specific standards and adhere to legal and ethical guidelines.

  • What types of cases do Sydney private investigators handle?

    Sydney private investigators handle various cases, including infidelity investigations, corporate investigations, missing persons cases, background checks, asset tracing, and fraud investigations.

  • What kind of evidence can a private investigator collect?

    A private investigator can collect various forms of evidence, including photographic and video surveillance, financial records, witness statements, and public records, depending on the case's nature and legal requirements.

  • How long does a private investigation take in Sydney?

    The duration of a private investigation varies depending on the case's complexity and the resources required. Simple tasks might take a few days, while more complex investigations can take several weeks or longer.

  • How can I find a reliable private investigator in Sydney?

    Researching online reviews, checking licensing and credentials, and speaking with potential investigators to assess their experience, expertise, and communication style are all crucial steps in finding a reliable private investigator.

  • Can a private investigator in Sydney work outside of Sydney?

    Many Sydney-based private investigators can and do work outside of Sydney, even across Australia or internationally, depending on their resources and the scope of the investigation.

  • How do I know if I need a private investigator?

    You may need a private investigator if you suspect infidelity, have concerns about fraudulent behavior, need to locate a missing person, or require assistance with gathering evidence for a legal case.

  • Is all information shared with a private investigator confidential?

    Yes, all reputable private investigators adhere to strict confidentiality protocols to protect their clients' sensitive information.
