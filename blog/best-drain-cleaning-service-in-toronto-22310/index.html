    FAQ: Best Drain Cleaning Service in Toronto

    1. What are the signs I need a drain cleaning service in Toronto?

      Common signs include slow draining sinks or tubs, gurgling sounds from drains, persistent unpleasant odors, and water backing up into your sinks or toilets.

    2. How much does drain cleaning typically cost in Toronto?

      The cost of drain cleaning varies depending on the severity of the blockage and the method used. It's best to get an upfront quote from the service provider. Tornado Plumbing & Drains offers transparent pricing, so you know what is involved.

    3. What methods do drain cleaning services use?

      Common methods include drain snaking, hydro-jetting, and camera inspections. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to clear blockages, and camera inspections allow plumbers to see inside your pipes.

    4. How often should I have my drains cleaned?

      For preventative maintenance, it's generally recommended to have your drains cleaned every 1-2 years, or sooner if you experience recurring issues. Regular drain cleaning helps prevent clogs from forming.

    5. Do Toronto drain cleaning services offer emergency services?

      Yes, many services, including Tornado Plumbing & Drains, offer 24/7 emergency drain cleaning services to address urgent issues.

    6. What can I do to prevent drain clogs?

      Avoid pouring grease or oil down the drain, use drain strainers, and flush your drains regularly with hot water. Consider having a professional inspection of your drain lines.

    7. What areas of Toronto does Tornado Plumbing & Drains serve?

      Tornado Plumbing & Drains services all of Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area (GTA).

    8. Is hydro-jetting safe for my pipes?

      When performed by a professional, like those at Tornado Plumbing & Drains, hydro-jetting is safe and effective for most types of pipes, including those made of PVC, copper, and cast iron. They will assess your pipes before performing the service.

    9. How do I choose the right drain cleaning service in Toronto?

      Consider factors such as reputation, experience, customer reviews, service offerings, pricing, and whether they offer 24/7 emergency service. Also, look for services like video inspections that show exactly what is causing the clogs. Finally, search for transparency in pricing and whether they deliver on their promises.

      Pay attention to comments about responsiveness, professionalism, the quality of work, and how they handle any issues that might arise.
