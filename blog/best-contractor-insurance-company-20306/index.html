FAQ

  • What does contractor insurance cover? Contractor insurance typically covers general liability, workers' compensation, commercial auto, and professional liability, providing financial protection against accidents, injuries, property damage, and professional errors.
  • Why is contractor insurance important? Contractor insurance is essential to protect your business from financial ruin due to lawsuits, accidents, or property damage. It helps you meet contractual obligations and demonstrates professionalism to clients.
  • How much does contractor insurance cost? The cost of contractor insurance varies depending on factors such as the type of work performed, the size of the business, the number of employees, and the coverage limits.
  • How can I find the best contractor insurance rates? Compare quotes from multiple insurance providers, assess your specific insurance needs, and review your coverage options carefully to find the best rates.
  • What is general liability insurance for contractors? General liability insurance protects contractors from claims of bodily injury or property damage caused by their business operations, providing financial support.
  • What is professional liability insurance for contractors? Professional liability insurance (also known as errors and omissions insurance) protects contractors from claims of financial loss due to errors in professional services.
  • Do I need workers' compensation insurance? If you have employees, workers' compensation insurance is generally required by law. It covers medical expenses and lost wages for employees injured on the job.
  • What types of contractors need insurance? Nearly every type of contractor needs insurance, including electricians, plumbers, carpenters, HVAC technicians, and general contractors.
  • How do I file a claim? Contact your insurance provider immediately after an incident or loss. Follow their claims process, providing all requested documentation and cooperating with their investigation.
  • Can I customize my contractor insurance policy? Yes, most insurance providers allow you to customize your policy to meet your business's specific needs, including adjusting coverage limits and adding endorsements.
  • Where can I find the best insurance for my contracting business? You can find the best insurance by researching reputable insurance providers and consulting with professionals who specialize in contractor insurance like The Contractor's Choice Agency.
