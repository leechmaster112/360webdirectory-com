Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some frequently asked questions about wedding florists in Pittsburgh:

  1. How far in advance should I book a Pittsburgh wedding florist? It's recommended to book your florist as early as possible, ideally 9-12 months out, or even sooner for peak wedding seasons like spring and fall 2025.
  2. What questions should I ask potential wedding florists in Pittsburgh? Ask about their experience, style, pricing, availability, and process. Inquire if they work within your budget, can deliver the vision, what the specific arrangements may look like, how many weddings they will take on that year, and additional related costs.
  3. What factors influence the cost of wedding flowers in Pittsburgh? Factors include the types of flowers, complexity of arrangements, size of the wedding party, and level of customization, and seasonal availability of certain blooms.
  4. Do Pittsburgh florists offer consultations? Most reputable florists in Pittsburgh offer consultations. This is a great opportunity to present your ideas, and get their professional feedback.
  5. Can I provide inspiration photos to my Pittsburgh wedding florist? Absolutely! Inspiration photos are highly encouraged and are a great way to communicate your desired aesthetic. This will give the florist an idea about your design preferences.
  6. What types of flowers are best for a Pittsburgh wedding? Popular choices in Pittsburgh include roses, peonies, hydrangeas, and seasonal blooms. Local florists help source the best arrangements for all seasons.
  7. Do Pittsburgh florists offer delivery and set-up services? Many florists offer full-service options, including delivery, setup, and teardown services and can set up at venues in the area. Confirm these services as part of your arrangements.
  8. What happens if my preferred flowers are not available? Reputable florists will provide alternatives to offer similar looks as a substitute. Discuss your options with them in advance.
  9. How can I make sure my wedding flowers stay fresh throughout the event? Consider choosing seasonal blooms and discuss options like floral preservation or refrigeration with your florist. They should make recommendations specific to your event.
  10. Do Pittsburgh florists offer packages or custom design options? They offer both. Some have pre-designed packages, others offer full custom options.
  11. When should I expect to pay the balance? Review your contract, most florists require deposits with the balance due before the wedding.
  12. Is it possible to have a sustainable floral design? Yes, ask your florist about eco-friendly practices.
