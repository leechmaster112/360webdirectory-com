Frequently Asked Questions

  1. What is paint protection film (PPF)?

    Paint protection film, also known as PPF or clear bra, is a transparent thermoplastic urethane film applied to painted surfaces of a new or used vehicle to protect it from stone chips, scratches, and UV damage.

  2. How long does PPF last?

    High-quality PPF, when properly installed and maintained, can last for 7-10 years, providing long-term protection for your vehicle.

  3. Does PPF self-heal?

    Many modern PPF films feature self-healing properties, meaning that minor scratches and swirl marks disappear over time with heat exposure, such as from the sun or hot water.

  4. How much does PPF cost in Vancouver?

    The cost of PPF in Vancouver varies depending on the coverage area (partial vs. full wrap), the type of film used, and the complexity of the installation. Prices can range from a few hundred dollars for partial coverage to several thousand for a full vehicle wrap.

  5. What areas of my car can be protected with PPF?

    PPF can be applied to nearly any painted surface of your vehicle, including the hood, front bumper, fenders, side mirrors, door edges, rocker panels, and even the roof.

  6. How is PPF installed?

    PPF installation involves thoroughly cleaning and preparing the vehicle's paint surface, precisely cutting the film to fit the vehicle's panels, and carefully applying the film to the surface, smoothing out any bubbles or imperfections.

  7. Can PPF be removed?

    Yes, PPF can be removed, although it's best to have it removed by a professional to avoid damaging the paint underneath. The removal process typically involves applying heat to soften the adhesive and peeling the film away.

  8. What is the best PPF brand?

    Several reputable PPF brands are available, including Xpel, STEK, and 3M. The "best" brand often depends on individual preferences and specific requirements, such as self-healing properties, clarity, and warranty.

  9. How do I choose a PPF installer in Vancouver?

    When selecting a PPF installer in Vancouver, consider factors like the installer’s experience, the quality of the films they use, their reputation, customer reviews, and the warranty they offer. Seek out installers with certified technicians and a strong attention to detail.

  10. Will PPF change the appearance of my car?

    High-quality PPF is designed to be virtually invisible, preserving the original look of your car's paint. Some films even enhance the gloss and shine.
