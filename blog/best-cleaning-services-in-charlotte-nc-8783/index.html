Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most popular cleaning service in Charlotte, NC?

Based on our research and customer reviews, Carolina Dream Clean is a top-rated and highly popular cleaning service in Charlotte, NC. They offer a wide range of services and consistently receive positive feedback.

How much does it cost to hire a house cleaning service in Charlotte?

The cost of house cleaning services in Charlotte varies depending on the size of your home, the services you require (e.g., standard cleaning, deep cleaning), and the frequency of service. Prices can range from $100 to $400+ per session, and it’s best to get a quote from several companies. Prices also depend on what you need cleaned, such as bathrooms, kitchens, bedrooms, and general areas.

What types of cleaning services are available in Charlotte?

Many cleaning services in Charlotte, NC offer a wide variety of services. These include standard house cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, commercial cleaning, office cleaning, and specialized services like window cleaning and post-construction cleanup. Be sure to explain your wishes and needs when you ask for your quote.

How do I choose the right cleaning service in Charlotte?

When choosing a cleaning service, consider your specific needs, budget, and preferences. Read reviews, compare services, inquire about cleaning methods and products (such as eco-friendly options), and check for insurance and bonding. This approach helps ensure service effectiveness.

Are cleaning supplies included in the price?

Most cleaning services in Charlotte provide their own cleaning supplies and equipment. However, it's always a good idea to confirm this when getting a quote or discussing your cleaning plan.

What areas of Charlotte do these cleaning services cover?

Most cleaning services in this list and others operate throughout the greater Charlotte metropolitan area. However, it's always best to confirm with the specific service to ensure they cover your exact address or neighborhood, such as Ballantyne, SouthPark, or Uptown. Be sure to ask about all service areas.

Do cleaning services in Charlotte offer any guarantees?

Many cleaning services offer some form of guarantee, such as a satisfaction guarantee. This means that if you're not happy with the service, they will come back and re-clean the areas in question. Check the specific service’s policy for details.

What should I do to prepare my home for a cleaning service?

To prepare for a cleaning service, you can tidy up clutter, pick up any items from floors, and secure any valuable or fragile items. This helps the cleaners work more efficiently and ensures the best possible results. When possible, open windows when they are cleaning to facilitate additional air circulation.
