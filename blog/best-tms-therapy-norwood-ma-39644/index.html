FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions About TMS Therapy in Norwood, MA

  1. What is TMS therapy, and how does it work?

    TMS therapy, or Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation, is a non-invasive treatment that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. These pulses target areas associated with mood regulation, such as in the treatment of depression and anxiety. It aims to restore normal brain function and alleviate symptoms.

  2. Is TMS therapy effective for depression?

    Yes, TMS therapy has proven effective in treating major depressive disorder. Studies have shown significant improvement in symptoms for many patients. TMS is often considered when other treatments like medication or therapy have not been fully effective.

  3. Are there any side effects of TMS therapy?

    TMS therapy typically has minimal side effects. The most common side effects are mild headaches or scalp discomfort during or after the treatment sessions. These side effects are usually temporary and subside quickly.

  4. How many TMS sessions are typically required?

    The typical TMS therapy course of treatment involves daily sessions, five days per week, for about 4-6 weeks for an initial treatment course, and maintenance sessions may be needed. The exact number of sessions varies depending on the individual's needs and response to treatment.

  5. How long does a TMS session last?

    Each TMS session usually lasts between 30 to 60 minutes. The duration can vary based on the specific treatment protocol used.

  6. Does insurance cover TMS therapy?

    Many insurance companies now cover TMS therapy, particularly for treatment-resistant depression. However, coverage can vary depending on the insurance provider and the individual's plan. It's recommended to check with your insurance provider to verify benefits.

  7. How do I find the best TMS therapy provider in Norwood, MA?

    When choosing a provider, consider factors such as experience, technology, and patient reviews. Look for a clinic that offers comprehensive services, a supportive environment, and personalized treatment plans, like the ones at Onward Psychiatry.

  8. What should I expect during a TMS therapy session?

    During a TMS session, you'll sit comfortably while a trained technician places a magnetic coil near your head. The coil delivers short magnetic pulses. You'll hear clicking sounds and may feel a tapping sensation on your scalp. You remain awake and alert throughout the session.

  9. Is TMS therapy painful?

    TMS therapy is generally not painful. Some patients may experience mild discomfort at the treatment site, such as a headache or scalp tingling, but these effects are usually brief.

  10. Can TMS therapy be combined with other treatments?

    Yes, TMS therapy can often be combined with other treatments, such as medication and psychotherapy. Your treatment plan should be tailored to your specific needs.
