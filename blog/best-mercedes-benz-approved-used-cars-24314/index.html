Frequently Asked Questions

What does "approved used" mean in the context of Mercedes-Benz?

An "approved used" Mercedes-Benz typically undergoes a rigorous inspection process by certified technicians, meeting specific quality standards set by the manufacturer or authorized dealerships. These vehicles often come with a warranty, providing buyers with added peace of mind and protection against potential issues.

Are used Mercedes-Benz cars reliable?

Mercedes-Benz vehicles are known for their quality and engineering, but the reliability of a used car depends on factors such as its service history, mileage, and the care it has received. Buying an "approved used" car helps to mitigate these factors as they will have been inspected and any necessary work completed.

What is the typical warranty on a Mercedes-Benz approved used car?

Warranty coverage varies depending on the specific program, dealership, and the age/mileage of the vehicle. It's common to find warranties that cover between 1 and 2 years, or a specific mileage, providing protection against unforeseen mechanical or electrical failures.

How can I verify the history of a used Mercedes-Benz?

Request a vehicle history report. This report provides valuable information, including accident history, service records, and title issues. Also, ask for any available documentation. Finally, have a mechanic inspect the vehicle to identify any potential problems.

What is the best way to finance a used Mercedes-Benz?

Explore financing options from various sources, including banks, credit unions, and the dealership. Consider the interest rates, terms, and any associated fees. Always compare offers to ensure you secure the most advantageous financing package.

Are Mercedes-Benz approved used cars more expensive than regular used cars?

Yes, approved used cars typically cost more than similar vehicles sold privately or through less stringent programs. This is due to the added benefits, such as comprehensive inspections, warranties, and guaranteed quality. The initial cost difference will often be offset by fewer repair costs and the peace of mind.

What should I look for when inspecting a used Mercedes-Benz?

Examine the vehicle's exterior for any signs of damage, such as dents, scratches, or rust. Check the interior for wear and tear, and ensure all features function correctly. Check under the hood for any leaks or unusual noises and request a test drive. Most importantly, consult the service history to make sure the car has been regularly maintained.

Can I negotiate the price of an approved used Mercedes-Benz?

Yes, while the prices might be firmer than with non-approved vehicles, there's often still room for negotiation. However, pricing will depend greatly on the available stock in your local market. Do your research to determine the fair market value of the vehicle before making an offer.
