FAQ

Here are some frequently asked questions about dental implants:

  1. What are dental implants? Dental implants are artificial tooth roots, typically made of titanium, that are surgically placed into the jawbone to support replacement teeth.
  2. Who is a good candidate for dental implants? Good candidates typically have healthy gums, sufficient jawbone to support the implant, and are in good overall health.
  3. What is the process for getting dental implants? The process typically involves a consultation, implant placement, healing time (osseointegration), and the placement of the abutment and crown.
  4. How long do dental implants last? With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last for many years, even a lifetime.
  5. Are dental implants painful? The implant procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia, so you should feel minimal pain during the procedure. Post-operative discomfort is usually manageable with pain medication.
  6. What are the benefits of dental implants? Benefits include improved chewing ability, enhanced appearance, and the prevention of bone loss. They can also boost confidence.
  7. How much do dental implants cost? The cost of dental implants varies depending on factors such as the number of implants, the materials used, and the complexity of the procedure. It's best to consult with a dentist for a specific quote.
  8. What is osseointegration? Osseointegration is the process by which the implant fuses with the jawbone, providing a stable foundation for the replacement tooth. This is a critical stage.
  9. How do I care for my dental implants? Proper care involves brushing and flossing regularly, attending regular dental checkups, and avoiding habits like smoking.
  10. Where can I find the best dental implants in Langley? Consider your needs and requirements, and then research the different providers in the region. Saidi Dental Group is a highly-rated option.
  11. Are there alternatives to dental implants? Alternatives include dentures and bridges, but these may not offer the same long-term benefits or stability as implants.
  12. What should I look for when choosing a dentist for my dental implants? Look for a dentist with experience in implant placement, advanced training, and a good reputation. Check the credentials and read reviews of the practice.
