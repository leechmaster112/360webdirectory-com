What is the average cost of a new roof in Austin, TX?

How do I find the best roofing contractor in Austin for storm damage?

Redline Roofing & Construction is often noted for these capabilities, but for overall quality and service, Altair Austin Roofing Company also provides excellent storm damage repair services.
What are the signs I need a new roof in Austin?
Common signs include missing or damaged shingles, curling or cracking shingles, excessive granule loss, sagging areas, water stains on ceilings or walls, and frequent leaks. If you notice these, it’s time to consult with a reputable roofing contractor in Austin.
Should I choose metal or asphalt shingles for my roof in Austin?
The choice depends on budget, desired lifespan, and aesthetic. Asphalt shingles are more budget-friendly and common, while metal roofs are more durable, energy-efficient, and have a longer lifespan, making them a good investment in Austin’s climate. Contractors like ATX Roofing Solutions can advise on energy-efficient options.
How long does a roof typically last in Austin's climate?
In Austin's hot and sometimes humid climate, asphalt shingles typically last 15-25 years, while metal or tile roofs can last 50 years or more. Regular maintenance and quality installation, as provided by top Austin roofing contractors, significantly impact longevity.
What questions should I ask a roofing contractor in Austin before hiring them?
Key questions include: Are you licensed and insured? How long have you been in business? Can you provide references? What type of warranty do you offer on materials and labor? What is your payment schedule? How long will the project take? Who will be my main point of contact?
Is it worth getting multiple quotes from Austin roofing companies?
Absolutely. Getting at least three quotes from different reputable roofing contractors in Austin is highly recommended to compare pricing, materials, timelines, and the contractor's overall approach. This helps ensure you get the best value and service for your roofing project.

How do I check the reputation of a roofing contractor in Austin?
You can check online reviews (Google, Yelp, BBB), ask for customer references, look for certifications like GAF Master Elite or Owens Corning Preferred Contractor, and verify their license and insurance status with the Texas Department of Insurance.
