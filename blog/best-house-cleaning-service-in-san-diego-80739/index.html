What is a "deep clean" versus a regular house cleaning in San Diego?

A regular house cleaning typically involves basic tasks like dusting, vacuuming, mopping, and cleaning high-touch surfaces in bathrooms and kitchens. A deep clean is much more intensive, incorporating tasks such as cleaning inside appliances (oven, refrigerator), behind furniture, baseboards, window sills, ceiling fans, and light fixtures. Services like Bravo Maids offer both options.

Can I find eco-friendly house cleaning services in San Diego?

Yes, there are several eco-friendly house cleaning services in San Diego, such as Green Leaf Cleaning Services and EcoShine Cleaning Services. Many other companies, including Bravo Maids, also offer green cleaning options using non-toxic, biodegradable products upon request.

How much should I tip my house cleaner in San Diego?

Tipping is generally appreciated but not always mandatory. A common guideline is to tip 10-20% of the service cost for excellent service. Some clients choose to give a larger tip for exceptional cleaning or during holidays.

What if I'm not satisfied with the cleaning service in San Diego?

For one-time or deep cleaning services, booking two to three weeks ahead is wise to secure your preferred date and time.
