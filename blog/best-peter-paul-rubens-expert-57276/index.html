An attribution expert should possess a deep understanding of Rubens's stylistic evolution, workshop practices, and the nuances of distinguishing between original works, workshop productions, and copies. A strong academic background and significant publication record are good indicators.

Are auction house specialists reliable Peter Paul Rubens experts?

Yes, specialists at major auction houses have extensive practical experience with Rubens's works, market values, and attribution through handling numerous pieces. Their expertise is generally reliable, especially concerning marketability and condition.

What is the role of a provenance researcher in relation to a Rubens artwork?

A provenance researcher traces the complete ownership history of a Rubens painting, providing crucial information about its past, including any notable owners or potential legal claims, which is essential for establishing authenticity and value.
