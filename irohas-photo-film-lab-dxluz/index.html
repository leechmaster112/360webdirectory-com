Location

Irohas Photo Film Lab, located at 398-402 Sussex St, Haymarket, NSW, 2000, stands as a premier destination for discerning film photographers in the heart of Sydney's vibrant CBD. We are more than just a processing lab; we are a dedicated hub for film enthusiasts seeking exceptional results, unwavering professionalism, and a deep understanding of the photographic darkroom process, both traditional and modern. Nestled in the historic Haymarket precinct, Irohas Photo Film Lab provides a unique space where analogue passion meets contemporary expertise, offering a comprehensive range of film processing and printing services tailored specifically for our valued Haymarket clientele.

At Irohas Photo Film Lab, we pride ourselves on our commitment to quality and consistency. Our state-of-the-art equipment and meticulously calibrated processes ensure that your film captures the full dynamic range and tonal richness it was designed for. Whether you're a professional shooter requiring precise colour accuracy or an aspiring artist exploring the nuances of black and white, we provide the technical support and creative guidance to bring your vision to life. Our location at 398-402 Sussex St makes us easily accessible for photographers working within the city centre, providing a convenient point of call for all your film development needs.

Our core service revolves around film processing. We handle a wide variety of film formats, including 35mm, medium format (4x5, 4x6, 5x4, 6x6, 6x7, 6x9, 220, 120, 110), and large format (4x5, 8x10). We process films in our temperature-controlled laboratory using high-quality chemical solutions sourced specifically for optimal results. Our processing times are designed for efficiency without compromising quality, ensuring your images are handled with the utmost care from the moment they arrive until they leave.

Alongside film processing, we offer a range of finishing options to enhance your photographs. These include choice of scan output (slide film and positive scans, negative scans), various print sizes and paper types (matte and gloss photographic papers, different weight options), and custom framing services. We understand the desire to preserve memories and showcase artistic work, and our Haymarket studio provides the space and equipment to do just that. We can also provide custom cutters and mounting services for your prints.

For photographers who prefer the timeless feel of a traditional darkroom print, we offer C-41 colour print development alongside our black and white processing. We can develop your C-41 colour prints in-house, ensuring colour accuracy and consistency. We also provide traditional black and white print processing (using both Caffenol and standard chemical processes) for those seeking the classic darkroom experience. Our commitment to analogue extends to providing expert advice on film selection, loading techniques, and shooting strategies to help you get the most out of your photographic journey.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What film formats do you process at your Haymarket location?

A1: We process a wide range of film formats at our 398-402 Sussex St location, including 35mm, all medium format roll films (4x5, 4x6, 5x4, 6x6, 6x7, 6x9, 220), and large format films (4x5, 8x10). We handle both colour negative (C-41) and black and white films.

Q2: How long does it typically take to process film and receive prints at Irohas Photo Film Lab?

A2: Standard film processing takes 2 business days. Rush processing (1 business day) is available for an additional fee. Print turnaround times depend on the size, paper choice, and quantity, but standard black and white prints are typically ready within 1-2 business days after processing. Please check our website or contact us directly for specific turnaround times for your order.

Q3: Can I drop off my film and pick it up at your Haymarket studio?

A3: Yes, we offer both drop-off and collection services from our location at 398-402 Sussex St. You can drop off your film during our operating hours, and we will contact you when your processed film and prints are ready for collection. Alternatively, we can arrange delivery within the Sydney CBD (Haymarket area) for a fee.

Q4: Do you offer custom framing services at your Sydney CBD location?

A4: Yes, we provide custom framing services for your photographs at our Haymarket studio. We offer various frame types and mounting options. Please inquire about specific framing requirements and associated costs when placing your order.

Q5: Can you provide advice on film types or shooting techniques?

A5: Absolutely. We are passionate about film and can offer expert advice on film selection, shooting techniques, and basic darkroom procedures. While we primarily focus on processing and printing, our team is knowledgeable and happy to discuss analogue photography and help you make informed decisions for your projects.
