Struggling with persistent drain blockages in your Sydney home or business? Wilco Relining offers a revolutionary solution: trenchless pipe relining using advanced UV curable technology. Based right here in Sydney, Australia 2000, we specialise in restoring damaged and blocked pipes without the need for disruptive excavation.

Traditional pipe repair methods often involve digging up floors, gardens, or driveways, causing significant disruption, mess, and potential damage to your property. At Wilco Relining Sydney, we eliminate these problems entirely. Our process involves inserting a flexible liner impregnated with UV curable resin into your existing pipe. Once in place, we use a powerful UV light to instantly cure the resin, creating a strong, seamless, and highly durable pipe within your pipe.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What exactly is pipe relining?

A: Pipe relining, also known as trenchless pipe rehabilitation, is a process where a flexible liner saturated with resin is inserted into your existing damaged pipe. Once in place, it's cured (often using UV light) to create a new, seamless pipe within the old one.

Q2: Is UV pipe relining suitable for all types of pipes?

A: Wilco Relining Sydney primarily uses UV curable resin technology, which is highly effective on a wide range of pipe materials including clay, asbestos cement, concrete, and plastic (HDPE, PVC, GRP). Our team will assess your specific pipe type to ensure relining is the best solution.

Q3: How long does a typical relining job take?

A: The actual relining process inside the pipe is typically very quick, often completed within a few hours. However, the entire job, including site preparation, assessment, and curing time, can vary depending on the pipe's condition and length. Our team will provide you with a clear timeline.

Q4: Is the relining process safe?

A: Absolutely. Wilco Relining Sydney adheres to strict safety protocols. The UV curing process is quick and uses minimal light exposure, and our team is fully trained and equipped to ensure a safe working environment for everyone.

Q5: What kind of warranty do you offer?

A: We stand behind the quality of our work. Wilco Relining Sydney typically offers a warranty on our pipe relining installations, reflecting our confidence in the durability and performance of the new pipe created using our advanced technology. Please inquire about our specific warranty details when you contact us.
