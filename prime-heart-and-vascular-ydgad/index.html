Prime Heart & Vascular - Expert Cardiology and Vascular Care in Plano

Located at 6201 Dallas Parkway, Suite 210, Plano TX 75024, Prime Heart & Vascular is dedicated to providing comprehensive and compassionate cardiovascular care to the residents of Plano. Our state-of-the-art facility is staffed by highly skilled cardiologists and vascular specialists who are committed to diagnosing, treating, and preventing heart disease and vascular conditions.

Cardiovascular health is fundamental to overall well-being. Prime Heart & Vascular understands the unique needs of our Plano patients and offers a full spectrum of services designed to address heart and vascular concerns at every stage. We emphasize preventive care, early detection, and personalized treatment plans to ensure the best possible outcomes for our community.

Our cardiovascular services include:

Comprehensive Cardiology Care: This includes preventive cardiology, diagnostic testing (such as stress tests, echocardiograms, and electrocardiograms), management of heart failure, coronary artery disease, arrhythmias, and hypertension. Vascular Disease Evaluation and Treatment: We diagnose and manage conditions affecting the blood vessels, including peripheral artery disease, carotid artery disease, and venous disorders. Interventional Procedures: Our team performs minimally invasive procedures like coronary angioplasty and stenting, and vascular interventions to restore blood flow and alleviate symptoms. Preventive Services: We offer risk assessments, lipid management, and lifestyle counseling to help Plano residents maintain a healthy heart and prevent cardiovascular diseases.

At Prime Heart & Vascular, we believe in a patient-centered approach. Our Plano-based team takes the time to listen to your concerns, explain your condition in understandable terms, and develop a treatment plan tailored to your specific needs and goals. We are committed to utilizing the latest diagnostic technologies and evidence-based treatments to provide the highest standard of care for cardiovascular health in Plano.

Whether you are experiencing symptoms, have a family history of heart disease, or are seeking routine cardiovascular check-ups, our Plano location is here to help. We provide convenient access to expert care for all your heart and vascular health needs.
