Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What exactly does air duct cleaning involve? A: Our process typically includes a thorough inspection of your ductwork, followed by powerful vacuuming and brushing to remove accumulated dust, debris, and contaminants from the interior surfaces of your ducts, plenums, and accessible registers and grilles.

Q2: How often should I get my air ducts cleaned? A: While recommendations can vary, many industry standards suggest professional air duct cleaning at least once every 3-5 years for typical residential settings. However, factors like pets, smoking, renovations, or visible mold/dust buildup may necessitate more frequent cleaning.

Q3: Can dirty ducts really make me sick? A: Yes. Accumulated dust, mold, mildew, and other allergens in dirty ducts can be circulated throughout your living or working space, potentially triggering or exacerbating allergies, asthma, and other respiratory conditions.

Q4: Will air duct cleaning help my HVAC system run better? A: Absolutely. Clean ducts allow for unrestricted airflow, reducing the strain on your system's fan and motor. This can lead to improved energy efficiency, lower utility bills, and potentially extend the lifespan of your heating and cooling equipment.

Q5: Are your cleaning methods safe and effective? A: Yes. We use EPA-approved, high-efficiency filtration systems and industry-standard cleaning techniques to ensure a safe and effective cleaning process. Our equipment is designed to capture 99.97% of particles down to 0.3 microns, providing a healthy and clean environment right here in Dallas.
