Location

Our mission is simple: to provide exceptional music education and a wide range of musical products all within one convenient location. We believe that music should be accessible, enjoyable, and constantly evolving. That's why we've created a space that combines the nurturing environment of a music school with the comprehensive offerings of a dedicated music store.

At our St Kilda studio, our experienced instructors offer tailored music lessons for all ages and abilities. We cater to a diverse range of interests, including popular music styles like Rock, Pop, and Jazz, as well as classical offerings such as Piano, Violin, Cello, and Guitar. We focus on building a strong foundation while encouraging creativity and personal expression. Our lessons are designed to be fun and engaging, ensuring students stay motivated on their musical journey.

Our store provides everything you need to support your musical passion. We stock a comprehensive range of new and used acoustic and electric guitars, bass guitars, ukuleles, pianos (both acoustic and digital), violins, violas, cellos, and other essential string instruments. Finding the perfect instrument or gear to suit your style and budget is our goal.
