Mikroautobusų nuoma Vilniuje - Movie Wheels

Movie Wheels has been part of the filmmaking and advertisement industry in Lithuania since 2013. Based in Vilnius, we saw how much productions struggled without proper transport. To solve this, in 2016 we launched our specialized minibus and van rental services, together with equipment rental, to make film production smoother and faster. Today, Movie Wheels operates a fleet of 53 purpose-built minibuses and vans, adapted for filmmaking needs across Vilnius and other Lithuanian cities. Our vehicles support costume and make-up departments, camera and props teams, art and DIT crews, and many other production units. Each vehicle is equipped with practical solutions like tail lifts, wardrobe racks, and workstations. For smaller projects, we also offer minibuses and vans that can be driven with just a B category driver’s licence, making them accessible for both large productions and independent creators. With more than 3,531 rentals completed and 4,425 active days on set, Movie Wheels has become a trusted partner for film crews, commercial shoots, and individual drone operators across Lithuania. Our Services Minibus and van rental – A wide selection of vehicles in different sizes, tailored for filmmaking tasks, from wardrobe and make-up trucks to cargo and passenger transport. Additional equipment – Cordless tools, electric appliances, cleaning gear, and other essentials to support production work. Individual projects – Custom transport and equipment solutions for atypical or special filming needs. Our Fleet Movie Wheels provides a diverse range of vehicles, including Mercedes Benz Sprinters, Iveco Daily, Renault Masters, Volkswagen Crafters, Opel Vivaro, and Fiat Ducato. Our fleet also features mobile toilets, generators, wardrobe and make-up buses, office-on-wheels units, and DIT vans, ensuring that every department’s requirements are covered on set. Wherever your production takes place — in Vilnius, Kaunas, Klaipėda, Šiauliai, Panevėžys, or any other Lithuanian city — Movie Wheels delivers reliable, flexible, and production-focused minibus and van rental services that keep your crew moving and your project on schedule.
