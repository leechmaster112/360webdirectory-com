Frequently Asked Questions

What subjects do you tutor?

Apex Tuition Australia offers comprehensive tutoring across a wide range of subjects, including but not limited to English, Mathematics (from basic numeracy to advanced calculus), Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Humanities (History, Geography, Economics, Politics), Languages (including preparation for IELTS/TOEFL), and various university entrance exam preparation. Our tutors specialise in specific subjects, ensuring you receive expert guidance.

How do I book a tutoring session?

Booking is simple! You can visit our website https://www.apextuitionaustralia.com/, contact us via phone at 0405 532 487, or inquire through our Google Business Profile. We will discuss your needs, match you with a suitable tutor, and arrange the date, time, and mode of tuition (online or in-home).

Are your tutors qualified?

Absolutely. Our tutors are highly qualified professionals, typically holding advanced degrees (Master's or Bachelor's) in their respective fields or possessing significant industry experience. They undergo a rigorous selection process to ensure they possess not only subject matter expertise but also excellent teaching skills and a genuine passion for helping students succeed.

Do I need to pay upfront for sessions?

We offer flexible payment options. You can typically book sessions in advance and pay for them either online securely via our website or over the phone. We strive to make the payment process straightforward and convenient for our clients.

Can I cancel or reschedule a session?

Yes, we understand that plans can change. We require reasonable notice for cancellations or rescheduling to allow for rebooking opportunities. Please contact us directly at 0405 532 487 as soon as possible if you need to make changes to your booking.
