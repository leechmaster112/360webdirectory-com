Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What kind of images do your 3D/4D ultrasounds produce?

A: Our advanced ultrasound technology produces detailed, high-resolution 3D images that can be viewed as still pictures or as a 4D video showing your baby's movements in real-time. These images allow parents to see facial features, body details, and sometimes even subtle expressions.

Q2: Is the 3D/4D ultrasound safe for my baby?

A: Yes. The 3D/4D ultrasound technology we use at Chubby Cheeks operates at the same safe power level as traditional 2D ultrasound imaging, which is commonly used for all routine prenatal care. We adhere to the highest safety standards recommended by governing medical bodies.

Q3: How early can I get a 3D/4D ultrasound at your Pittsburgh studio?

A: We typically recommend scheduling your 3D/4D ultrasound appointment between weeks 14 and 26 of pregnancy for the best image quality. While we can sometimes accommodate earlier viewings, optimal visualization usually occurs later in the second trimester.

Q4: Do you offer gender determination with your 3D/4D ultrasounds?

A: Our 3D/4D ultrasound technology allows for accurate gender determination in most cases after 14 weeks of gestation, provided there are no obstructions. We are happy to share this information as part of the experience.

Q5: Can I bring family and friends to my appointment at your studio?

A: Absolutely! We encourage sharing this special experience with loved ones. Our studio provides a comfortable waiting area, and we welcome family members to observe from the waiting room while you have your private scanning session.
