Our Services at Dr. Majd Al Habbab Dental Clinic (Villa 30, Sheraouh Street, Al-Waab, Doha):

* Comprehensive General Dentistry: We provide a wide array of fundamental dental services to maintain your oral health, including regular dental check-ups, professional teeth cleaning (dental prophylaxis), personalized oral hygiene guidance, and the application of dental sealants and fluoride treatments for both children and adults.

* Advanced Restorative Dentistry: We restore damaged or decayed teeth with precision. Our services include durable and natural-looking dental fillings, the reconstruction of damaged teeth using state-of-the-art dental crowns, and the secure placement of essential dental bridges to replace missing teeth.

* Effective Endodontic Care: For issues affecting the innermost part of your tooth, the tooth pulp, we offer expert root canal treatment (endodontics) to save your natural tooth and alleviate pain, preserving your smile and oral function.

* Fixed Prosthodontics & Cosmetic Dentistry: We enhance both the function and appearance of your smile. This includes the fabrication and placement of aesthetically pleasing porcelain veneers to mask minor imperfections, the restoration of single or multiple missing teeth with lifelike dental implants, and the construction of custom dental crowns and bridges for a perfect fit and natural look.

* Dental Implants: We specialize in providing stable and permanent solutions for missing teeth through dental implant procedures. Using advanced techniques, we place biocompatible titanium implants into the jawbone, providing a secure foundation for replacement teeth like crowns, bridges, or dentures, restoring your ability to eat comfortably and confidently.

* Pediatric Dentistry: We are committed to fostering good oral health habits from a young age. Our clinic offers specialized care for children, including gentle dental examinations, child-friendly teeth cleanings, and preventive treatments tailored to ensure a positive dental experience for your little ones living locally.
