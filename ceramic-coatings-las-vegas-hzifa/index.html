FAQ

1. What is the primary service performed at your Henderson location?

Our primary service at our facility located at 7585 Commercial Way suite g is the professional application of automotive ceramic coatings. This process involves meticulously preparing your vehicle's paint and then applying a liquid polymer that chemically bonds with the factory paint, creating a incredibly hard, protective layer with exceptional hydrophobic properties.

2. How long does the ceramic coating application take at your Henderson shop?

The entire process is completed exclusively at our address on Commercial Way and typically takes 2-3 days for a standard sedan or coupe. This extensive time frame is necessary to ensure we perform a flawless paint correction and allow the coating to properly cure in a controlled environment for maximum durability and performance.

3. How do I maintain my ceramic coating after the service is completed?

Maintenance is straightforward. We provide all clients with specific aftercare instructions and recommend using pH-neutral soaps and detail sprays designed for coated vehicles. The hydrophobic nature of the coating means dirt and water rinse away easily, significantly reducing the need for frequent washing. We advise against automatic brush car washes to preserve the coating's integrity.

4. Why is paint correction necessary before applying the coating at your facility?

Paint correction is a mandatory first step performed at our shop because scratches, swirls, or imperfections present on the paint's surface. Our process of machine polishing ensures the surface is perfectly smooth and flawless before sealing it with the coating, guaranteeing a pristine, glossy finish.
