Expert Vascular Care

Our vascular specialists focus on the diagnosis and treatment of diseases affecting the blood vessels outside the heart – the arteries and veins. This includes conditions such as peripheral artery disease (PAD), carotid artery disease, renal artery disease, aneurysms, and varicose veins. We utilize minimally invasive techniques whenever possible to provide effective treatment with reduced recovery time and discomfort.

Common vascular procedures performed at our Allen location include:

Angioplasty and Stenting (for narrowed arteries) Atherectomy (removal of plaque) Carotid Stenting Varicose Vein Treatments (laser ablation, sclerotherapy) Venous Graft and Bypass Care Management of Aortic Aneurysms

We emphasize preventative vascular care as well, educating patients on risk factors and promoting healthy habits to maintain vascular health and prevent disease.
