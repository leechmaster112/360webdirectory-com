Location

The SoundLab Music School & Store, located at 1 Chapel St, St Kilda VIC 3182, is more than just a music store; it's a dedicated hub for musical education and exploration right in the heart of St Kilda, under the iconic Astor Theatre.

Our mission is simple: to help you turn up your musical ability. Whether you're a complete beginner picking up the guitar for the first time, a dedicated hobbyist looking to refine your skills, or a young aspiring musician eager to take lessons, The SoundLab Music School provides expert guidance in a welcoming environment.

Our experienced instructors are passionate musicians committed to helping students of all ages and abilities unlock their potential. We offer a diverse range of music lessons covering popular instruments like guitar, bass, drums, keyboard, and voice, as well as other options like ukulele, mandolin, and various other popular instruments. We also cater to those interested in learning music theory, songwriting, and composition. Our curriculum is designed to be fun, engaging, and tailored to individual learning paces and goals.

But learning doesn't stop at the doorstep of our music school. As part of The SoundLab Music Store, we provide a comprehensive range of services right here in St Kilda. We offer a wide selection of new and used musical instruments and equipment, from beginner-friendly starter kits to professional-grade gear for experienced players. Our knowledgeable staff can help you find the perfect instrument or set up for your band or performance needs.

Our store also boasts a significant selection of top-quality audio equipment, including headphones, amplifiers, microphones, cables, and accessories. We cater to musicians, audio enthusiasts, and sound professionals alike, ensuring you have access to the tools needed for practice, recording, or live performance.

Need expert technical support? Our store provides essential services like instrument setup, repairs, and maintenance. Keeping your gear in top condition is crucial, and our team is here to help you maintain your investment.

At The SoundLab Music School & Store, we believe music should be accessible and enjoyable. We are committed to serving the vibrant St Kilda community, providing high-quality music education and essential musical gear all under one roof. Discover your sound and elevate your musical journey with us right here in St Kilda.
