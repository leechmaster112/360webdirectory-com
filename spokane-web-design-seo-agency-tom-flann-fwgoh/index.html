FAQ

Q1: How long does it take to build a custom website?

A: The timeline for building a custom website varies significantly based on its complexity, the amount of content needed, and your responsiveness during the process. A simple brochure website might take 2-4 weeks, while an e-commerce site with multiple pages and features could take several months. We provide a detailed project plan and timeline once we begin your project.

Q2: Will my website appear on the first page of Google immediately after launch?

A: No website achieves immediate top rankings. Search engines require time to crawl and index new websites. Furthermore, achieving sustainable, high rankings involves ongoing optimization and earning quality backlinks, which takes time. Our process includes thorough SEO implementation from the start, but significant results typically take 3-6 months or longer, depending on your industry competitiveness and the scope of the SEO work.

Q3: How does local SEO specifically help my Spokane business?

A: Local SEO targets customers searching for businesses like yours in Spokane or nearby areas. We optimize your website and online profiles (like Google My Business) with location-specific keywords, encourage customer reviews, ensure accurate business information (NAP), and build local citations. This increases your chances of appearing in local search results, Google Maps packs, and mobile searches, connecting you directly with potential customers actively looking for your services or products in the Spokane area.

Q4: Do you offer ongoing website maintenance and support?

A: Yes, we offer various levels of ongoing website maintenance and support packages. This typically includes regular software updates (WordPress, themes, plugins), security monitoring, malware scanning, content updates, and performance optimization. Maintaining your website is crucial for security, speed, and functionality, and we can tailor a plan to fit your budget and needs.

Q5: Can you help me with social media marketing?

A: While our primary focus is web design and SEO, we have a good understanding of digital marketing principles and can advise on basic social media strategies to complement your website and SEO efforts. We can help you define your goals, choose the right platforms, and develop simple content calendars. For comprehensive social media management, we can recommend trusted partners or provide guidance on tools you can manage yourself.
