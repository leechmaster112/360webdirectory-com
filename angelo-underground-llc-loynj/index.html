Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What types of excavation services do you offer in Portland?

Angelo Underground LLC provides a comprehensive range of excavation services tailored to Portland's needs. This includes residential excavation for home foundations, landscaping, and site prep; commercial excavation for building projects and infrastructure; utility trenching for water, sewer, gas, and electrical lines; site grading and remediation; and specialized services like pond or swimming pool excavation.

How do I request a quote for excavation or trenching work?

Requesting a quote is simple! You can call our office directly at (360) 721-0265, where our team will listen to your project requirements and discuss potential costs. Alternatively, you can visit our website at https://angelounderground.com/ and use our online contact form to provide project details for an initial assessment. We strive to provide accurate quotes based on the specifics of your job.

Are your excavation and trenching services compliant with local regulations?

Absolutely. Angelo Underground LLC operates with a strong emphasis on safety and regulatory compliance. We are fully aware of and adhere to all local, state, and federal regulations governing excavation and trenching in Portland. This includes obtaining necessary permits, following safe digging practices (often coordinating with "Call Before You Dig" services), and ensuring our work meets or exceeds industry standards.

What is your process for handling trenching projects?

Our trenching process begins with a site assessment and consultation to understand your project goals and plan the trench route and specifications. We then prepare the necessary equipment and site access. During the trenching phase, we utilize appropriate machinery for efficiency and safety, constantly monitoring depth and alignment. We perform all required utility locates before digging. Upon completion, we backfill the trench according to specifications, ensuring proper compaction, and clean up the site thoroughly.

Do you offer emergency excavation services?

While we primarily focus on scheduled projects, Angelo Underground LLC understands that unforeseen circumstances can arise. In the event of an emergency requiring excavation services, such as a collapsed structure or sudden utility exposure, we are committed to responding promptly and safely to address the situation. Please call our office directly during business hours or, in true emergencies, contact local authorities first.
