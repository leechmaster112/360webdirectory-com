Basin Plumbing

Basin Plumbing is a locally owned and operated plumbing company based in Odessa, Texas, that serves the entire Permian Basin. They offer a wide range of residential and commercial plumbing solutions, prioritizing customer service, upfront pricing, and professional workmanship. Emergency Plumbing: They provide a 24/7 emergency service, so customers can get help for urgent issues like burst pipes or severe clogs at any time. Water Heater Services: This includes the installation, repair, and replacement of both traditional tank water heaters and high-efficiency tankless models. They can diagnose and fix common problems like leaks, strange noises, and inconsistent water temperature. Drain Cleaning and Repair: Basin Plumbing uses advanced tools like hydro-jetting and camera inspections to clear stubborn clogs and ensure drains are flowing freely. They can also repair or replace damaged drain lines. Water and Sewer Line Services: They specialize in the installation, replacement, and repair of both water and sewer lines. This includes trenchless plumbing, which allows for underground pipe work with minimal disruption to a property's landscape. Gas Line Services: They provide professional installation and replacement of gas lines for homes and businesses, with a strong focus on safety and adherence to regulations. Medical Gas Installation: For healthcare facilities, they offer specialized services for installing and maintaining medical gas systems, which are critical for delivering gases like oxygen and nitrous oxide. General Plumbing Repairs and Installations: Their team of licensed plumbers can handle a variety of general plumbing needs, including fixing leaky faucets and pipes, repairing or replacing fixtures, and installing new plumbing for remodels or new construction projects. They also offer water softener installation and repair and can address issues with septic systems.
