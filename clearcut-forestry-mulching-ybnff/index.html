What exactly is forestry mulching?

Forestry mulching is a land-clearing technique that uses specialized machinery to shred and grind vegetation, like brush and small trees, into a layer of mulch. This process effectively clears land while leaving nutrient-rich organic matter behind, promoting soil health.
