Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Paige Dentistry offer? We provide a comprehensive range of dental services including preventative care (check-ups, cleanings, X-rays), restorative treatments (dental crowns, bridges, fillings, root canals, dentures), cosmetic dentistry (teeth whitening, veneers, Invisalign), and emergency dental care.

Are you accepting new patients? Yes, we welcome new patients! Please contact our office at (705) 560-4000 to schedule your initial consultation. During this visit, we can discuss your dental history, current needs, and schedule any necessary diagnostic procedures.

What is the cost of dental treatment? Our fees are based on standard industry rates and are transparently communicated before any treatment begins. We offer various payment options and accept all major dental insurance plans. You can find more detailed fee information on our website or by calling our office.

Do you offer emergency dental care? Absolutely. Unforeseen dental emergencies can happen at any time. While we strive to accommodate walk-in patients during regular business hours, we understand that emergencies require prompt attention. Please call our office at (705) 560-4000 during business hours, or seek immediate attention at an emergency room or dental clinic after hours.

How often should I schedule dental check-ups? We generally recommend scheduling regular dental check-ups and professional cleanings every six months. However, the frequency may vary depending on your individual oral health needs, risk factors, and existing dental conditions. Your personalized dental plan will outline the recommended schedule during your initial visit.
