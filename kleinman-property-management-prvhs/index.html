Location

Kleinman Property Management, located at 5201 East River Road, Suite 308, Fridley, MN 55421, brings over nine decades of expertise in Minnesota property management. We are dedicated to providing exceptional service to property owners across the Twin Cities and surrounding areas, ensuring their real estate investments are managed professionally and effectively.

For over 95 years, Kleinman Property Management has been a trusted name in the industry. Our commitment is to simplify property management for our clients, allowing them to focus on other priorities while we handle the complexities of owning and maintaining rental properties. We pride ourselves on a client-focused approach, utilizing our extensive experience to deliver tailored solutions that meet the unique needs of each property and its owner.

Our Services encompass every aspect of property management, designed to provide seamless oversight and maximize the value of your asset:

Residential Property Management: We specialize in managing single-family homes, duplexes, townhomes, and multi-family units. Our comprehensive services include finding qualified tenants, handling lease agreements, managing day-to-day operations, overseeing maintenance and repairs, and ensuring compliance with all relevant regulations.

Tenant Relations & Screening: We pride ourselves on finding and retaining quality tenants through thorough screening processes. Our goal is to foster positive landlord-tenant relationships, ensuring smooth communication and addressing any concerns promptly to maintain harmonious living environments.

Maintenance & Repairs: Kleinman Property Management coordinates all necessary maintenance tasks, from routine upkeep and minor repairs to major renovations. We work efficiently to resolve issues quickly, minimizing disruption for both tenants and property owners.

Financial Management: We handle all financial aspects, including rent collection, budgeting, forecasting, and financial reporting. Our transparent accounting practices ensure you have a clear understanding of your property's performance and profitability.

Marketing & Leasing: Leveraging our local market knowledge, we develop effective marketing strategies to attract qualified tenants and achieve optimal rental rates for your property.

Property Inspections & Reporting: Regular inspections help maintain property standards and identify potential issues early. We provide detailed reports to keep you informed about the condition and performance of your investment.

Whether you own a single property or a portfolio, Kleinman Property Management offers the professionalism, dedication, and local expertise you need. Contact us today to learn how our 95+ years of experience can benefit your property management needs.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: How long has Kleinman Property Management been in business?

A: Kleinman Property Management has been providing property management services for over 95 years, bringing a wealth of experience and stability to property owners in the Twin Cities area.

Q: What types of properties do you manage?

A: We manage a wide range of residential properties, including single-family homes, duplexes, triplexes, townhomes, and multi-family units (four-plex and larger) primarily in the Twin Cities metro area.

Q: What is your tenant screening process?

A: Our tenant screening process is thorough and designed to find reliable, quality tenants. It typically includes credit checks, background checks, eviction history verification, and income verification to ensure candidates meet our leasing criteria and standards.

Q: How do you handle maintenance requests?

A: We prioritize maintenance requests. Tenants can submit repair requests through various channels, and our team works to schedule and complete necessary repairs promptly, keeping property owners informed throughout the process.

Q: What are your fees and contracts?

A: Our fee structure is competitive and transparent, typically based on a percentage of the monthly rent collected. We offer flexible management contracts tailored to the specific needs and scope of service for each client's property. We encourage you to review our detailed contract information on our website or discuss it directly with our team.
