What is TMS Therapy?

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain to regulate mood. Unlike traditional Electroconvulsive Therapy (ECT), TMS does not require anesthesia or electrical shocks. At MedPsych Integrated - Cary, we utilize this advanced technology to offer a safe and effective treatment option for patients seeking relief from treatment-resistant depression and other conditions where TMS is indicated.
