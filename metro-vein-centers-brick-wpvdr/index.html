Q2: Are the treatments at your Brick clinic painful?

A2: Patient comfort is a top priority at Metro Vein Centers Brick. We utilize local anesthesia and advanced techniques to ensure minimal discomfort during procedures. Most patients report only a mild sensation during treatment, and recovery is typically quick with minimal side effects.

Q3: How long does a typical vein treatment appointment take at your Brick facility?

A3: The duration of your visit depends on the specific treatment. Your initial consultation and evaluation may take about 30-60 minutes. The actual treatment procedure itself usually ranges from 30 minutes to a few hours, depending on the complexity and number of veins being treated.

A4: Recovery at our Brick location is generally straightforward. You may experience some mild soreness, bruising, or swelling, which our team will discuss with you beforehand. Most patients can resume light activities immediately after treatment, with full recovery typically taking one to two weeks, depending on the procedure.

Q5: Do I need a referral to schedule a vein treatment consultation at your Brick office?
