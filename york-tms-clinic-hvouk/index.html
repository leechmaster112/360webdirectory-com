Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation is a cutting-edge treatment that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. Specifically, it targets areas of the brain that are underactive in individuals with depression. The magnetic field generated by the TMS device is similar to that produced by an MRI machine and is delivered through a coil placed on the scalp. This non-invasive procedure does not require surgery or anesthesia, and most patients can resume their normal activities immediately after each session.

Our team at 1806 N George St, York, PA 17404 is comprised of experienced and compassionate mental health professionals dedicated to providing personalized care. The TMS therapy process at York TMS Clinic is designed to be comfortable and convenient. Each treatment session typically lasts between 20 to 40 minutes. Patients remain seated in a comfortable chair and can read, listen to music, or simply relax during the procedure. The magnetic pulses are delivered in a repetitive pattern, hence the term "Repetitive TMS" or rTMS, which is the most common form used for depression. Over the course of several weeks, these repeated stimulations help to rebalance brain activity, leading to a reduction in depressive symptoms.
