Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):

What is IV therapy?

IV therapy, or intravenous therapy, involves the administration of fluids, vitamins, minerals, and other nutrients directly into your bloodstream via an intravenous drip. This method bypasses the digestive system, allowing for significantly faster absorption and more effective delivery of nutrients compared to oral supplements.

How does IV therapy work?

During your IV therapy session, a healthcare professional will insert a small, sterile catheter (like a saline lock) into a vein in your arm. Nutrient-rich fluid is then slowly dripped directly into your bloodstream through an IV pump. The process is generally quick and comfortable, with most treatments taking between 30 minutes to an hour.

What conditions can IV therapy help with?

IV therapy can be beneficial for a wide range of situations, including boosting the immune system, alleviating fatigue and stress, enhancing athletic recovery, improving skin health, supporting detoxification, managing dehydration, and providing relief from symptoms associated with hangovers or jet lag.

Are the IV fluids and vitamins safe?

Absolutely. We use only sterile, pharmaceutical-grade solutions and follow strict safety protocols. Our team is professionally trained to administer IV therapy safely. We source high-quality ingredients to ensure the effectiveness and safety of our treatments.

Do I need a prescription for IV therapy?

No prescription is required for our wellness-focused IV drips and vitamin shots. These are considered cosmetic and wellness treatments. However, for certain specialized IV treatments or if you have underlying medical conditions, we may consult with your physician. Our knowledgeable staff will guide you on the appropriate treatments.
