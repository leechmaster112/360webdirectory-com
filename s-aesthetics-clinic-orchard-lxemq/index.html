Frequently Asked Questions

1. What types of injectable treatments do you offer at your Shaw Centre location?

We provide a range of injectable treatments including Botox and Dysport for wrinkle reduction, as well as various dermal fillers like Juvederm and Restylane to enhance facial volume and contours.

2. Are your laser skin treatments safe?

Yes, our laser treatments are performed by experienced practitioners using advanced technology in a controlled environment. Safety is a top priority, and we customise treatments to suit individual skin types and concerns.

3. How long does a typical Botox treatment session take?

A Botox session usually takes about 15-30 minutes, depending on the areas being treated. You can expect immediate results, with full effects becoming visible within a week.

4. Do you offer consultations before any procedure?

Absolutely. We believe in a thorough consultation process where we discuss your goals, assess your skin, and recommend the most suitable treatment plan tailored to you.

5. What should I expect during a chemical peel treatment?

During a chemical peel, a specially formulated solution is applied to the skin to exfoliate the outer layers. You may experience mild stinging or warmth, but the procedure is generally comfortable. Post-treatment, you might notice some redness and peeling, which are normal parts of the recovery process.
