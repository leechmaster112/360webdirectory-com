Q2: How long do I have to file a personal injury claim in Omaha, Nebraska?

A2: In Nebraska, the statute of limitations for personal injury claims is generally five years from the date the injury occurred or the date you discovered the injury. It is crucial to consult with our attorneys at Rensch & Rensch Law in Omaha as soon as possible to ensure your claim is filed within this timeframe.
