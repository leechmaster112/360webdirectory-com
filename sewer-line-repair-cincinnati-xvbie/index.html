Q2: How do you inspect sewer lines without digging?

A2: We utilize advanced sewer camera inspection technology. Our team inserts a high-definition camera into your sewer line through an existing access point (manhole or cleanout). The camera transmits real-time video footage to our monitor, allowing us to accurately diagnose the problem's location and severity without digging.

Q3: What are the most common causes of sewer line problems in Cincinnati homes?

A3: Common causes for sewer line issues in Cincinnati homes often include tree root intrusion (especially older clay pipes), pipe deterioration (cracks, corrosion, shifting), grease and foreign object clogs, ground shifting, and improper disposal of non-flushable items. Our local Cincinnati team is experienced in identifying these specific issues.

A4: We offer both sewer line repair and replacement services. Our team will assess your specific situation using camera inspection and other diagnostic methods.
