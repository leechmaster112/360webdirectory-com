Location

The Ruby On Johnson, nestled right here on the vibrant heart of Johnson Street in Victoria, BC, welcomes you to a space where creativity meets comfort. We're more than just a restaurant; we're a community hub, a place to gather, share a laugh, and fuel your day with delicious, inventive food and expertly crafted drinks.

Nestled at 642A Johnson St, Victoria, BC V8W 1M6, our cozy and stylish interior is designed to be a reflection of the fun and energy that Johnson Street embodies. It's a place where friends reconnect, locals linger over coffee and conversation, and everyone feels like they've found their spot. Our menu is built around the beloved concept of brunch, but we don't stop there. We offer a selection of lunch and dinner options throughout the week, ensuring there's always something delicious to satisfy your cravings.

At The Ruby On Johnson, we pride ourselves on sourcing high-quality, locally-inspired ingredients whenever possible. Our creative culinary team puts a unique spin on classic brunch favourites and offers exciting menu items that showcase flavour and texture. Picture fluffy stacks of pancakes drizzled with decadent sauces, perfectly poached eggs atop vibrant salads, crispy and tender pastries, and innovative takes on breakfast burritos and bowls. For the sweet tooth, our dessert menu features indulgent treats that are sure to please.

Complementing our food are our handcrafted cocktails, signature mocktails, and a carefully curated selection of coffee and espresso beverages. Our baristas are passionate about their craft, ensuring every cup is brewed perfectly. Our drinks menu offers refreshing options for those looking for something lighter, as well as sophisticated choices for evening enjoyment.

Whether you're catching up with friends for lunch, planning a date night, hosting a small gathering, or simply enjoying a solitary treat, The Ruby On Johnson provides the perfect setting. Our atmosphere is relaxed yet lively, funky yet welcoming. We encourage you to step through our doors and experience the warmth and creativity that defines us.

Find us easily at 642A Johnson St, Victoria, BC V8W 1M6. You can explore our full menu and learn more about our offerings on our website: https://therubyvictoria.com/johnson/. For reservations or any inquiries, feel free to call us directly at (778) 265-8750.

Frequently Asked Questions

What days and times do you offer brunch?

Our classic brunch menu is typically available on weekends, starting from mid-morning. Weekdays might feature a more limited brunch selection or a different menu focus. Please check our website https://therubyvictoria.com/johnson/ for the most current weekday and weekend brunch offerings.

Do you offer catering services for events held off-site?

Yes, we love supporting local events! While primarily focused on serving our neighbourhood at 642A Johnson St, we also offer catering services for private events held elsewhere in Victoria. Please contact us via phone at (778) 265-8750 to discuss your catering needs.

Is The Ruby On Johnson accepting reservations?

Absolutely! We recommend making a reservation, especially for weekends or larger groups, to ensure we can accommodate you. You can reserve your table online via our website https://therubyvictoria.com/johnson/ or by calling our phone number (778) 265-8750.

What is your bar/drink policy like?

We have a fully stocked bar with a wide range of craft cocktails, wines, beers, and non-alcoholic beverages. We encourage responsible drinking and a fun atmosphere. Our focus is on providing quality drinks to complement our food.

Do you have a patio or outdoor seating?

Yes! We offer outdoor seating options, allowing our guests to enjoy the beautiful weather and soak in the atmosphere of Johnson Street. Our patio seating is available seasonally, subject to weather conditions and local bylaws.
