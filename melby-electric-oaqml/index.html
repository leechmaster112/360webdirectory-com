The company offers a comprehensive range of electrical services to meet the needs of any home or business. They also specialize in critical system upgrades, including electrical panel and circuit breaker replacements to ensure safety and efficiency. Serving their home base of Marysville, Melby Electric's service area extends across all of Snohomish County, reaching into South Skagit and North King counties as well. This broad coverage allows them to assist a wide range of customers with everything from small household repairs to significant commercial electrical work. Recognizing that electrical issues can arise unexpectedly, the company also offers 24/7 emergency services to provide prompt and reliable assistance whenever it is needed, solidifying their reputation as a dependable local contractor.
