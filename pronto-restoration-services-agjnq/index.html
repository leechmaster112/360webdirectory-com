The Pronto Restoration Process

Our process is designed to be thorough and efficient, ensuring comprehensive restoration at our Frisco facility:

1. Emergency Assessment and Response:

Upon receiving your call, we dispatch our team immediately. We understand the urgency and aim to be on-site as quickly as possible from our Lebanon Road location in Frisco. Our technicians begin by visually inspecting the affected area to assess the scope of the damage. We identify the source of the water intrusion and take immediate steps to stop the leak or further water entry. This crucial first step prevents the damage from escalating and helps protect your property.

2. Water Extraction:

Once the source is identified and contained, our powerful industrial-grade water extraction equipment comes into play. We utilize submersible pumps, wet vacuums, and commercial-grade extractors to remove standing water from floors, carpets, and other surfaces. This step is vital to prevent secondary damage and the growth of mold and mildew.

3. Drying and Dehumidification:

Removing standing water is only the first part of the battle. Thorough drying is essential to prevent mold and mildew growth and to restore structural integrity. Our advanced moisture detection tools, including moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras, allow us to accurately measure humidity levels and identify hidden moisture within walls, floors, and ceilings. We deploy powerful commercial dehumidifiers and air movers strategically throughout your space to accelerate the drying process, ensuring your Frisco property dries completely and thoroughly.

4. Cleaning and Sanitizing:

After the area is completely dry, we proceed with cleaning and sanitizing. We remove residual odors and contaminants, ensuring your space is restored to a clean and safe condition. Our cleaning solutions are effective against bacteria, viruses, and other harmful microorganisms, providing peace of mind.

5. Reconstruction and Restoration:

Depending on the extent of the damage, our skilled team may need to repair or replace damaged drywall, flooring, cabinetry, or other structural elements. We work meticulously to restore your space to its pre-loss condition, using quality materials and techniques.
