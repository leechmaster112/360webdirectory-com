Q3: How often should I schedule maintenance for my HVAC system?

A: For optimal performance and longevity, we recommend scheduling preventative maintenance for your HVAC system at least once per year. Many customers opt for twice-yearly service (spring and fall) to ensure their system is ready for the heating and cooling seasons. Regular maintenance helps maintain efficiency, prevent unexpected breakdowns, and protect your investment.

Q4: Can you help me choose the right size HVAC system for my home?

A: Absolutely. Proper sizing is crucial for effective and efficient HVAC performance. Our experienced technicians can conduct a thorough assessment of your home's square footage, layout, insulation levels, window types, and other factors to accurately determine the right system size for your specific needs. Choosing the correct size ensures optimal comfort and avoids the inefficiency of oversized or undersized units.

For repairs, diagnostic fees and part costs will depend on the problem. For replacements, costs are influenced by the chosen system type, brand, size, and labor expenses.
