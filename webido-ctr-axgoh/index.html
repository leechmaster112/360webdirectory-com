What is Click-Through Rate Optimization?

Click-Through Rate (CTR) is the percentage of people who click on your ad after seeing it. It's calculated by dividing the number of clicks by the number of impressions. A higher CTR generally signals to Google that your ad is relevant and appealing to users searching for your products or services. This positive signal often leads to improved ad rankings and potentially lower costs per click.
