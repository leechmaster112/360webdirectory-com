Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of aircraft do you offer for charter from your Honolulu location?

We offer a range of aircraft, including light, mid-size, and large cabin jets suitable for various passenger counts and trip durations. Our fleet is carefully selected and maintained to the highest standards.

Q2: How far in advance should I book a private jet flight from Honolulu?

While last-minute flights are sometimes possible depending on availability, we recommend booking as far in advance as needed, especially during peak travel seasons or for larger groups, to ensure your preferred aircraft and timing are available.

Q3: Can you fly me directly to other Hawaiian islands from Honolulu?

Yes, we can arrange direct private jet charters to all major Hawaiian islands from our Honolulu base, offering a faster and more comfortable alternative to commercial flights with fewer stops.

Q4: What is included in the price of a private jet charter?

Our quoted price typically includes the aircraft rental, block hours (fuel, crew, flight planning, etc.), and usually includes the services of a pilot and dispatcher. Specific inclusions can be confirmed during your booking.

Q5: How does the security process work for private jet travel from Honolulu?

The security process for private jets is generally more streamlined than commercial flights. We coordinate with the appropriate authorities to ensure a smooth and efficient arrival and departure experience for our clients.
