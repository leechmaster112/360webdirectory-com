Detroit Dog Park

Detroit Dog Park is a fully fenced, off-leash dog park at 2699 Rose St in Detroit. The park features spacious grassy areas with both shaded and sunny spots, picnic tables for owners, and is well-maintained by volunteers. Hours, photos, and directions are on its Doggie Park Near Me listing.
