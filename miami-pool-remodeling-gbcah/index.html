Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Do you only remodel existing pools, or do you also build new ones in New York?

We specialize in pool remodeling, which includes renovating, resurfacing, reconfiguring, and enhancing existing pools. While we primarily focus on transforming established pools, our expertise allows us to effectively work within the constraints and opportunities of existing structures typical in the New York area.

Q2: How does the remodeling process work for a New York property?

The process typically begins with a consultation to understand your vision and needs. We then create design options, discuss permits (which we can often assist with), and provide a detailed plan and timeline. Construction involves careful excavation, structural work if needed, resurfacing, and finishing touches, all managed according to New York building codes and regulations.

Q3: What types of pool styles do you remodel to fit New York backyards?

We offer a wide range of remodeling options suitable for various New York spaces. This includes classic rectangular designs, modern geometric shapes, luxurious Infinity pools (carefully integrated with drainage), multi-level designs, and specialized features like tiling, waterfalls, and integrated lighting. We focus on designs that complement the architectural style of your home.

Q4: How do you handle the unique aspects of pool remodeling in an urban New York setting?

We understand the specific challenges of urban environments, such as space limitations, existing landscaping, and adherence to city regulations. Our team is experienced in working within these constraints, utilizing efficient construction methods and ensuring minimal disruption to your property and neighborhood during the project.

Q5: What materials do you typically use for pool remodeling in New York?

We utilize high-quality, durable materials suitable for the New York climate and aesthetic preferences. Common choices include various types of tile (porcelain, glass, stone-effect), gunite (masonry) for structural flexibility, and precast concrete for faster installation. We also offer options like vinyl liner replacements and fiberglass resurfacing, recommending the best material based on your specific situation, budget, and desired longevity.
