Our Focus: Conditions Affecting the Lower Extremities

We treat a wide spectrum of vascular and venous conditions impacting the feet, ankles, and legs. Our expertise lies in managing issues related to the veins, which are essential for returning blood to the heart. When these systems malfunction, it can lead to significant discomfort and complications. Some of the common conditions we address at our 670 Glades Rd practice include:

Varicose Veins: These are enlarged, twisted veins that typically appear as bulging, rope-like structures, often blue or dark purple. They most commonly occur in the legs and can cause pain, aching, heaviness, swelling, and skin changes.

Spider and Telangiectasia Veins: These are smaller, surface-level veins that appear as red or blue web-like patterns or thin lines on the skin's surface, often on the face, legs, or ankles. While generally not a serious health risk, they can be cosmetically concerning.

Chronic Venous Insufficiency (CVI): A condition where the valves in the veins of the legs do not function properly, leading to blood pooling. This can cause swelling (edema), skin discoloration (often brownish), thickening (lipodermatosclerosis), and even skin ulcers (stasis dermatitis).

Edema: Swelling in the legs, ankles, and feet due to fluid accumulation, which can be a symptom of underlying venous disease or other systemic conditions.

Mixed Venous Malformations: A combination of different types of abnormal veins that may require specialized treatment planning.

Advanced Treatments Tailored to You

At Foot, Ankle & Leg Vein Center, we pride ourselves on offering cutting-edge, minimally invasive procedures performed by our skilled podiatric team at our 670 Glades Rd facility. Our commitment to innovation ensures that our patients receive the most effective and comfortable care available. We utilize techniques such as:

Venous Ablation: Using laser or radiofrequency energy to close off damaged or problematic veins, allowing blood to reroute through healthier vessels.

Sclerotherapy: Injecting a solution directly into spider veins or smaller varicose veins, causing them to fade away over time.

Laser Treatment: Utilizing targeted light energy to treat surface veins and reduce symptoms associated with venous insufficiency.

Compression Therapy: A crucial part of both diagnosis and treatment, helping to improve blood flow, reduce swelling, and support healing.

All treatments are customized to your specific condition, symptoms, and lifestyle, ensuring optimal results and a swift recovery. Our goal is always to minimize downtime and maximize patient comfort at our Boca Raton practice.
