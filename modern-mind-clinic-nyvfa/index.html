Location

At Modern Mind Clinic, we provide a modern approach to mental wellness, offering specialized treatments for those seeking advanced care. Our clinic is dedicated to delivering effective and innovative solutions, focusing on TMS Therapy, Spravato treatment, and comprehensive telehealth mental health services. Located at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, Southfield, MI 48075, our facility is designed to be a sanctuary of healing and progress, equipped with the latest technology to support your journey toward improved mental health.

Modern Mind Clinic specializes in Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) Therapy, a non-invasive treatment that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. This FDA-approved therapy is primarily used for treating Major Depressive Disorder, especially for individuals who have not found success with traditional antidepressant medications. Our TMS Therapy at our Southfield location is administered under the supervision of experienced medical professionals in a comfortable and private setting, ensuring each patient receives personalized and attentive care.

In addition to TMS, Modern Mind Clinic offers Spravato (esketamine) nasal spray treatment. This cutting-edge treatment is another FDA-approved option for treatment-resistant depression. Administered on-site at our Southfield clinic under strict medical supervision, Spravato works rapidly to help alleviate depressive symptoms. Our team carefully monitors each session to ensure the highest standards of safety and comfort, making the experience as supportive and effective as possible for every patient.

Our telehealth services are conducted by our same team of dedicated clinicians, ensuring continuity of care and a seamless experience for all patients connected to our Southfield practice. FAQ

1. What is TMS Therapy and how is it administered at your Southfield clinic?

TMS Therapy is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate areas of the brain involved in mood regulation. At our Southfield location at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, patients relax in a comfortable chair while a specialized device is positioned near the head. Treatment sessions typically last around 20 minutes, and patients can return to their daily activities immediately afterward.

2. How does the Spravato treatment process work at Modern Mind Clinic?

Spravato treatment at our Southfield clinic involves self-administering a nasal spray under the direct supervision of our medical team. Following the administration, patients are monitored in our comfortable clinic for approximately two hours to ensure safety and comfort throughout the experience. This process is a key part of our specialized treatment offerings.

3. Are telehealth appointments available for all services at Modern Mind Clinic?

Our telehealth services are designed for psychiatric evaluations, therapy sessions, and follow-up care. However, TMS Therapy and Spravato treatments require in-person visits at our Southfield clinic due to the specialized equipment and medical supervision necessary. Our team can provide more details on what appointments can be conducted remotely.

4. How do I schedule a consultation at your Southfield location?

Scheduling a consultation is simple. You can call our clinic directly at (248) 607-7190 to speak with our staff. We will guide you through the process and set up an initial appointment at our Southfield office to discuss your needs and determine the most appropriate treatment plan.

5. What should I expect during my first visit to your clinic in Southfield?

Your first visit to Modern Mind Clinic at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750 will involve a comprehensive assessment with one of our clinicians. This evaluation helps us understand your history and current challenges, allowing us to create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific goals for mental wellness.
