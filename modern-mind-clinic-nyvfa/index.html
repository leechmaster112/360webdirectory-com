Location

At Modern Mind Clinic, we provide advanced mental health treatments from our professional office at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, Southfield, MI 48075. Our clinic is dedicated to offering innovative and evidence-based solutions for individuals seeking to improve their mental well-being. We understand that the journey to mental wellness is personal, which is why our approach is tailored to meet the unique needs of each client who walks through our doors at our Southfield location.

The core of our practice at Modern Mind Clinic is our specialized TMS Therapy. Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation is a non-invasive treatment that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain, specifically targeting areas involved in mood regulation. This FDA-cleared treatment is a valuable option for those who have not found the desired results with traditional antidepressant medications. Our clinic is equipped with state-of-the-art TMS technology, and our experienced team administers this treatment in a comfortable and supportive environment right here in our Southfield office.

In addition to TMS, Modern Mind Clinic offers Spravato (esketamine) nasal spray treatment. This FDA-approved medication, for use in conjunction with an oral antidepressant, is designed for treatment-resistant depression. Administered under the direct supervision of our medical staff within our clinic at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, Spravato provides a new pathway to hope and healing for qualifying individuals. We ensure each session is conducted with the highest standards of safety and care, monitoring patients throughout their treatment.

Understanding the need for accessible and flexible care, Modern Mind Clinic also provides comprehensive telehealth mental health services. This allows us to extend our professional support to clients remotely, ensuring continuity of care and making our services more readily available. Whether from the comfort of our clinic or through a secure virtual connection, our commitment to providing high-quality, modern mental health care remains unwavering.

Our team at the Southfield clinic is comprised of compassionate and highly skilled professionals dedicated to your mental health journey. FAQ

What is TMS Therapy and how is it administered at your Southfield clinic?

TMS Therapy is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate brain cells associated with mood control. At our Modern Mind Clinic location at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, Southfield, MI 48075, you will sit in a comfortable chair while a magnetic coil is gently placed against your head. The treatment is administered by our trained staff in a private room within our clinic.

What should I expect during a Spravato treatment session at your office?

Spravato treatment is administered under medical supervision at our Southfield clinic. You will self-administer the nasal spray in our comfortable and private treatment room. Following administration, our clinical team will monitor you for approximately two hours to ensure your safety and comfort throughout the session.

How do I schedule a telehealth appointment with Modern Mind Clinic?

Scheduling a telehealth appointment is simple. You can call our clinic at (248) 607-7190 to speak with our staff. We will guide you through the process and provide all necessary information to connect with your provider virtually from a location of your choice.

Are the treatments at Modern Mind Clinic in Southfield covered by insurance?

Many insurance plans do provide coverage for TMS Therapy and Spravato treatment. Our administrative team at our Southfield location will work with you to verify your specific insurance benefits and outline any associated costs before you begin your treatment plan.
