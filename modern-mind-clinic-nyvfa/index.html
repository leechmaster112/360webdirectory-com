Location

Modern Mind Clinic is a premier mental health treatment center located at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, Southfield, MI 48075. We specialize in providing advanced, evidence-based psychiatric care for individuals seeking transformative results. Our clinic is dedicated to offering cutting-edge treatments in a modern, comfortable, and professional environment, focusing on innovative solutions for treatment-resistant conditions.

At our Southfield facility, we deliver specialized TMS Therapy, a non-invasive treatment that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. This FDA-approved therapy is primarily used to treat Major Depressive Disorder when traditional antidepressant medications have not been effective. Our TMS treatment is administered under the supervision of our expert clinical team in private treatment rooms designed for patient comfort and relaxation.

Our clinic also provides Spravato (esketamine) treatment, an FDA-approved nasal spray for Treatment-Resistant Depression. This breakthrough treatment is conducted in our carefully monitored suite, ensuring the highest standards of patient safety and care throughout the administration process and the required observation period. Our clinical staff provides compassionate supervision during each session.

Modern Mind Clinic offers comprehensive telehealth mental health services, allowing patients to connect with our providers remotely from the comfort of their own homes. Our telehealth platform ensures continuity of care, accessibility, and convenience for medication management and therapeutic consultations, all provided by our dedicated Southfield-based clinical team.

Every treatment plan at Modern Mind Clinic is personalized and developed through a thorough assessment by our medical professionals. We believe in a patient-centered approach, combining advanced medical treatments with compassionate support to help our patients achieve lasting mental wellness and improved quality of life.

To schedule a consultation or learn more about our services at our Southfield location, please contact us at (248) 607-7190 or visit our website at https://www.modernmindclinic.com/.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is TMS Therapy and how is it administered at your Southfield clinic?

TMS Therapy is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain to improve symptoms of depression. At our Modern Mind Clinic location at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, the treatment is administered while you sit comfortably in a chair. Each session lasts approximately 20-30 minutes, and you can return to your normal activities immediately afterward.

How does Spravato treatment work at your Southfield facility?

Spravato is an FDA-approved nasal spray for treatment-resistant depression. At our Southfield clinic, the treatment is administered under medical supervision in our dedicated treatment room. You will be monitored for approximately two hours after receiving each dose to ensure your safety and comfort throughout the process.

Can I receive telehealth services if I live near Southfield?

Yes, our Modern Mind Clinic provides telehealth mental health services to patients. These virtual appointments allow you to meet with our Southfield-based providers for medication management and consultations remotely, offering flexibility and convenience while maintaining the same standard of care.

What should I expect during my first visit to your clinic in Southfield?

During your initial visit to our clinic at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, you will undergo a comprehensive assessment with one of our medical professionals. This evaluation helps us understand your specific needs and determine whether TMS Therapy, Spravato treatment, or another approach is the most appropriate option for your situation.

How do I schedule an appointment at your Southfield location?

To schedule an appointment at Modern Mind Clinic, please call us directly at (248) 607-7190. Our team will guide you through the process, answer any questions you may have, and help you arrange a convenient time for your initial consultation at our Southfield office.
