Understanding NeuroStar TMS Therapy

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation uses a specialized device to generate a magnetic field through the skull and into the brain. This magnetic field creates a gentle tapping sensation on the scalp, stimulating nerve cells in the prefrontal cortex – an area of the brain crucial for regulating mood. Unlike medications, TMS targets specific brain regions without requiring ingestion, making it a non-systemic treatment.

The therapy is completely painless and does not involve any electrical current passing through the body. Most patients experience little more than a tapping sensation during their sessions. A typical course of treatment involves daily sessions, usually lasting about 20 to 45 minutes each, over a period of several weeks.
