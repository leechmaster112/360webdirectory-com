A: We handle a broad range of employment law matters for employees in Toronto, including unfair dismissal or constructive dismissal, breaches of employment contracts, workplace harassment and discrimination claims, failure to pay wages or benefits, workplace injuries, and issues related to workplace safety and health regulations.

Q2: How long do I have to file a complaint if I believe I was wrongfully dismissed?

A: In Ontario, employees generally have two years from the date they became aware of the dismissal or the act that led to it to file a lawsuit. However, strict time limits often apply to certain workplace rights issues, like unpaid wages or benefits, which may require prompt action. We advise contacting us as soon as possible to discuss your specific situation.

A: Yes, absolutely. Workplace harassment by anyone, including managers, supervisors, or colleagues, is a serious matter under Ontario law. We have experience handling cases where harassment originates from management and will work to ensure your workplace rights are protected.
