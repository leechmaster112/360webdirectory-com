Dog Park at Bob Woodruff Park

The Dog Park at Bob Woodruff Park is a double-gated, fenced, seven-acre off-leash area in Plano, Texas, with separate sections for small and large dogs. Shaded seating, water fountains, and waste stations make it a popular spot for dog socialization. Hours, photos, and directions are on its Doggie Park Near Me listing.

What makes the Dog Park at Bob Woodruff Park unique?

Our defining feature is the seven-acre, fully fenced, double-gated off-leash area, offering an unparalleled amount of space for dogs to exercise and play freely in Plano.

Is the entire seven acres an off-leash area?

Yes, the full seven-acre expanse within our designated park boundaries is a secured off-leash zone for your dog's enjoyment.

What is the purpose of the double-gated entry?

The double-gated system is a crucial safety feature, ensuring that dogs cannot accidentally run out of the park when other dogs or people are entering or exiting.

Can my dog play with other dogs here?

Absolutely. The spacious design and dedicated off-leash nature of the park are specifically intended to foster safe and enjoyable socialization between dogs.

Is there anything else to do at Bob Woodruff Park besides the dog park?

While our focus is the dedicated seven-acre dog park, Bob Woodruff Park itself offers other amenities, but the Dog Park at Bob Woodruff Park specifically provides the off-leash experience within its fenced perimeter.
