Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is a ceramic coating and how does it protect my car?

A ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that is professionally applied to the exterior of your vehicle. At our North Phoenix facility, we use Ceramic Pro, which chemically bonds with your car's factory paint, creating a permanent, ultra-hard layer of protection. This layer is resistant to UV rays, chemical stains, dirt, and minor abrasions, making your car easier to clean and keeping it looking new for years.

2. Why should I choose Ceramic Pro North Phoenix for my ceramic coating?

Our location at 2033 W North Ln #20 is staffed by certified and experienced technicians who are experts in the precise application of Ceramic Pro products. We take the time to properly prepare your vehicle's surface with paint correction to ensure a flawless foundation for the coating, guaranteeing the highest quality finish and maximum durability for your specific vehicle.

3. How long does the ceramic coating application process take at your Phoenix shop?

The entire process can take between one to three days, depending on the package you select and the condition of your vehicle. This time is necessary for a meticulous wash, decontamination, paint correction, and the careful application and curing of the ceramic coating layers to achieve a perfect, long-lasting result.

4. Do you offer any warranty on your ceramic coating services?

Yes. The Ceramic Pro coatings applied at our facility come with a strong warranty. The Ceramic Pro Gold package, for example, includes a lifetime warranty for cars and a 5-year warranty for motorcycles, which is fully transferable to future owners, helping to protect your vehicle's resale value. Specific warranty details will be provided with your service.

5. How do I maintain my ceramic coating after the service is complete?

Maintenance is simple. We recommend washing your vehicle with a pH-neutral shampoo, using the two-bucket method, and avoiding automatic car washes with harsh brushes. We also offer maintenance services and products at our 2033 W North Ln #20 location to help you keep your coating in optimal condition for the life of the warranty.
