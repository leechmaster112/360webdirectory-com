Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the primary service offered at your Phoenix location?

Our primary service at our facility at 2033 W North Ln #20 is the application of Ceramic Pro nano-ceramic coatings. This involves professionally applying a liquid polymer that chemically bonds with your vehicle's factory paint, creating an incredibly hard, permanent layer of protection that shields against UV damage, chemical stains, and minor scratches.

2. Why is paint correction necessary before applying a ceramic coating?

Paint correction is a crucial first step performed at our shop. Any imperfections like swirls or scratches locked under a ceramic coating will be permanently sealed in. Our process involves meticulously machine polishing the paint to a flawless finish, ensuring the ceramic coating bonds perfectly and results in a perfectly smooth, high-gloss surface.

3. How long does the ceramic coating application process take at your North Lane facility?

The entire process is comprehensive and cannot be rushed. Depending on the chosen package and the vehicle's condition, the service typically takes between 1 to 3 days. This allows our technicians ample time for the essential steps of decontamination, paint correction, and the precise application and curing of the Ceramic Pro layers.

4. How do I maintain my ceramic coating after the service is complete?

Maintenance is simple. We recommend washing your vehicle with a pH-neutral shampoo, using the two-bucket method to prevent swirls. We advise against automatic car washes with abrasive brushes. For optimal performance, we offer professional maintenance washes and top-up coatings at our Phoenix location to help extend the life and brilliance of your investment.
