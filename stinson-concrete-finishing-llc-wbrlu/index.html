Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of concrete finishing services do you offer at your Lancaster location?

We specialize in various concrete finishing techniques performed right here in Lancaster, including broom-finished surfaces for excellent traction (ideal for driveways and sidewalks), stamped concrete to mimic the look of natural stone, brick, or wood, polished concrete to reveal a beautiful, durable, and low-maintenance surface (often used indoors and for high-traffic outdoor areas), and traditional troweled finishes for clean, smooth surfaces suitable for patios and slabs.

Q2: Are you available for residential concrete projects in Lancaster?

Absolutely! We frequently work with homeowners in Lancaster on residential projects. This includes enhancing driveways, creating beautiful and durable walkways, building stylish patios, finishing pool decks, and even polishing interior floors. We understand the importance of these projects for your home and approach each residential job with the same care and attention to detail we apply to commercial work.

Q3: How long does a typical concrete finishing project take once you start?

The timeline for a concrete finishing project in Lancaster depends heavily on the specific project scope, weather conditions (especially for outdoor work), and the complexity of the finish. We always provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation after assessing your needs. Generally, the actual finishing work itself can range from a few days for smaller projects like a driveway to several weeks if weather delays or more complex finishes are involved. We strive for transparency and keep you informed throughout the process.

Q4: Do you handle the entire concrete project from start to finish, or just the finishing?

We primarily focus on the finishing aspect of concrete projects. However, we work closely with our clients and their chosen general contractors or concrete suppliers. We can provide expert advice on the best time to schedule the finishing work, the appropriate techniques for your specific needs, and ensure the finishing aligns perfectly with the underlying concrete pour. We are happy to guide you through the process and coordinate with your team.

Q5: What should I consider when preparing my concrete project for Stinson Concrete Finishing LLC?

Proper preparation is key to a successful finish. While we handle the finishing itself, we advise that the concrete base must be properly cured and prepared by the pourer before we begin work. This ensures the best possible bond and final result. During our consultation, we will discuss the specific preparation requirements for your project to ensure everything is set up correctly for a smooth and high-quality finish right here in Lancaster.
