Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of employment law issues do you handle for clients in Toronto?

A1: Marvin A. Gorodensky P.C. focuses exclusively on employment law matters for clients located in Toronto. This includes issues like wrongful dismissal, constructive dismissal, human rights complaints, workplace harassment, pay deductions, and disputes related to employment standards and workplace discipline.

Q2: How long do I have to file a claim after my employment ends?

A2: Time limits for employment-related claims in Ontario vary depending on the specific issue (e.g., notice period, human rights complaints, pay deductions). Generally, claims related to termination must be filed within two years from the date of dismissal. However, human rights complaints have a one-year deadline from the date the discrimination occurred. It's crucial to consult with us promptly to understand the specific time limits applicable to your situation.

Q3: Can you help me with a workplace harassment claim?

A3: Yes, if you have experienced harassment or a poisoned work environment in your Toronto workplace, Marvin A. Gorodensky P.C. can assist you. We can advise on whether a claim might be possible, help gather evidence, and represent you throughout the process.

Q4: Do I need a lawyer to handle a complaint about unpaid wages?

A4: While you can file a claim for unpaid wages directly with the Ontario Ministry of Labour, having legal representation can significantly strengthen your case and ensure you receive all the compensation you are entitled to under the Employment Standards Act. Our firm can guide you through the process and represent you.

Q5: What should I do if I receive an unfair termination notice?

A5: If you receive a termination notice you believe is unfair or insufficient, it is important not to sign it hastily. Contact Marvin A. Gorodensky P.C. immediately. We can review the notice, advise you on whether it complies with your employment contract and the Employment Standards Act, and discuss your options, which may include negotiating a severance package or pursuing a claim for wrongful dismissal.
