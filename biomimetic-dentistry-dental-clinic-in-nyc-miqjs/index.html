Q1: What exactly is Biomimetic Dentistry?

A: Biomimetic Dentistry is an approach that focuses on restoring teeth and gums in a way that mimics the structure, function, and appearance of natural teeth and gums. It prioritizes conservative treatment, preserving healthy tooth structure whenever possible, and using materials and techniques that integrate harmoniously with your existing oral environment.
