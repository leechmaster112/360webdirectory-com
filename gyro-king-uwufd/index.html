Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What are your hours of operation?

A1: We are open for lunch from 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM and dinner from 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM, Monday through Saturday. We are closed on Sundays.

Q2: Do you offer delivery services?

A2: Yes, we offer delivery for orders placed through our website or phone. Delivery availability may depend on your specific location within the Ogletown Stanton area.

Q3: Can I order for takeout?

A3: Absolutely! You can easily order takeout from our menu by calling 3027377744 or placing your order online at www.gyrokingde.net.

A5: We accept cash, credit cards, and debit cards in-store. For online orders, we also accept major credit cards and digital payment options.
