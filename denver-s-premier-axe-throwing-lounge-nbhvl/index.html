Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is the age requirement to participate in axe throwing at your Denver location?

Our Denver location requires participants to be at least 18 years old to participate in axe throwing for safety and legal reasons. Minors are welcome in the lounge area but cannot participate in the throwing sessions.

Q2: How do I book a private event or group reservation at 1512 Larimer St?

Booking a private event or group reservation is easy. You can reserve your date by calling our phone number (720) 555-9367 or by booking online through our website https://axethrowinglounge.com/. We recommend booking in advance, especially for weekends or larger groups.

Q3: What safety measures are in place for axe throwing at your Denver venue?

At our Denver location, safety is our top priority. All our axes are meticulously inspected and maintained. Our certified range masters enforce strict rules, including mandatory safety glasses and proper footwear. The throwing area is clearly marked, and we ensure adequate supervision to guarantee a safe and fun experience for everyone.

Q4: Are there any food or beverage options available at the lounge?

Yes, our Denver lounge offers a selection of premium beverages, including craft cocktails, local beers, and curated wines. While we don't have a full-service kitchen, we provide a comfortable environment where you can enjoy drinks and snacks while you wait or relax before or after your axe throwing session.

Q5: Can I bring my own drinks or food into the venue?

We appreciate your support and allow outside food and non-alcoholic beverages for our private events and groups. However, outside alcohol is not permitted at our Denver location to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all guests.
