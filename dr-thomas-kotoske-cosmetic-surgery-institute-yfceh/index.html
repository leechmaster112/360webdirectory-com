Our Core Services at Body by Kotoske

While our focus is on surgical interventions, we also integrate carefully selected non-surgical options to complement our practice and provide a broader spectrum of rejuvenation possibilities for our patients residing in the Phoenix area.

1. Body Contouring & Liposuction:

Addressing concerns related to stubborn fat deposits and skin laxity is a cornerstone of our practice. Procedures like VASER® Liposuction and other advanced body contouring techniques allow us to sculpt specific areas of the body, such as the abdomen, thighs, buttocks, and arms, resulting in a more defined and proportionate silhouette. Dr. Kotoske designs each procedure meticulously to align with your body type and aesthetic goals.

2. Facelift and Neck Lift:

For those seeking to restore a more youthful facial appearance, our facelift and neck lift procedures are designed to lift, tighten, and redefine the jowls and neck contours. We employ techniques aimed at creating a natural rejuvenation that harmonizes with your facial features.

3. Breast Surgery:

We offer a spectrum of breast surgical services, including breast augmentation (using both implants and autologous fat transfer), breast lift (mastopexy), and breast reduction. Each procedure is customized to enhance your breast shape, size, and position, boosting your confidence and body image.

4. Eyelid Surgery (Blepharoplasty):

Removing excess skin, fat, and muscle from the upper and lower eyelids can dramatically open the eyes and create a more alert, youthful gaze. This procedure effectively addresses concerns related to drooping eyelids that can make one appear tired or older than they feel.

5. Facelift Combinations & Advanced Techniques:

Often, the most effective facial rejuvenation involves combining a facelift with other procedures, such as eyelid surgery or injectable fillers (used strategically for volume restoration). We may also incorporate techniques like a Thread Lift for a minimally invasive lift option or Thermi Tight® for skin tightening, depending on the desired outcome and patient preference.
