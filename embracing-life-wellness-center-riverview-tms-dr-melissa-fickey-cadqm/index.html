Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is TMS therapy and how is it performed at your Riverview clinic?

TMS therapy is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain to improve symptoms of depression. At our Riverview location at 6332 US-301, the patient sits comfortably in a chair while an electromagnetic coil is placed gently against their scalp. The treatment is administered in sessions that last about 20-40 minutes, during which the patient is awake and alert, and can resume normal activities immediately afterward.

2. Who is a candidate for TMS therapy at your wellness center?

TMS therapy is typically for adults diagnosed with Major Depressive Disorder who have not experienced satisfactory improvement from previous antidepressant medication. A consultation with Dr. Melissa Fickey at our Riverview office is necessary to conduct a thorough evaluation and determine if TMS is the right treatment option for your specific situation.
