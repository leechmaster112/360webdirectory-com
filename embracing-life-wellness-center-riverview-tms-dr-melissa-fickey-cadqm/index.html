Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is TMS therapy and how is it performed at your Riverview location? Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain to improve symptoms of depression. At our clinic at 6332 US-301, you will sit comfortably in a chair while a magnetic coil is gently placed against your scalp. The treatment is administered by our trained clinical team under the supervision of Dr. Fickey, and you can return to your daily activities immediately afterward.

2. Who is a good candidate for TMS treatment at your center? TMS therapy is typically recommended for adults who have been diagnosed with Major Depressive Disorder and have not experienced satisfactory improvement from previous antidepressant medications. The best way to determine if you are a candidate is to schedule a consultation with Dr. Melissa Fickey at our Riverview office for a comprehensive evaluation.

3. What types of psychiatric services does Dr. Fickey offer besides TMS? In addition to TMS, Dr. Melissa Fickey provides a full spectrum of psychiatric care, including diagnostic assessments, medication management, and integrative psychotherapy. All services are personalized and provided at our Riverview clinic to create a cohesive treatment strategy for each patient.

4. How do I schedule an appointment at the Embracing Life Wellness Center? Scheduling an appointment is simple. You can call our office directly at (813) 662-5919 to speak with our friendly staff. We will guide you through the process and set up your initial consultation at our location at 6332 US-301, Riverview, FL 33578.

5. Do you accept insurance for treatments at your Riverview clinic? We work with a variety of insurance providers to make our services accessible. Our team at the Riverview location can verify your specific mental health benefits and provide detailed information on coverage for TMS therapy and other psychiatric services during your initial phone call or consultation.
