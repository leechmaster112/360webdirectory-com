What areas do you service?

How does the mobile detailing process work?

It's simple! You can contact us via phone or our website to schedule an appointment. We'll discuss your detailing needs and preferences. On the day of your appointment, our professionally trained detailer will arrive on time, set up their mobile station, and meticulously work on your vehicle right at your location.

What sets Swankies Elite apart from other detailers?

We stand out through our unwavering commitment to quality, our use of premium detailing products, and our experienced, reliable team. Our mobile service offers unparalleled convenience, and we focus on customer satisfaction, ensuring every vehicle receives the personalized attention it deserves. We also offer various package options for different levels of service.

Do you offer any guarantees on your work?

Yes, we stand behind the quality of our work. We offer a satisfaction guarantee on our detailing services. If you are not completely satisfied with the results, please contact us, and we will work with you to ensure your experience meets your expectations.

How long does a typical detailing job take?

The duration of a detailing job depends on the type and extent of service required. Basic exterior washes and wax jobs typically take 1-2 hours. More comprehensive interior and exterior detail packages can range from 3-6 hours or more, depending on the vehicle's condition and the specific services selected.
