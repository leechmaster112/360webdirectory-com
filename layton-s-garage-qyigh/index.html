Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What types of vehicles do you service?

We service all makes and models, from classic cars to the latest innovations in automotive technology. Our ASE-certified technicians have experience with virtually every type of vehicle on the road today.

Q: How often should I get an oil change?

Modern vehicles typically require oil changes every 5,000 miles or 6 months, whichever comes first. However, consult your owner's manual for specific recommendations based on your vehicle's requirements.

Q: Do you offer tire services?

Yes, we provide comprehensive tire services including installation, rotation, balancing, and seasonal tire changes. Our team can help you choose the right tires for your driving needs.

Q: What's included in a standard multi-point inspection?

A standard multi-point inspection includes checking fluids, lights, brakes, tires, belts, hoses, and other critical components. This thorough evaluation helps identify potential issues before they become major problems.

Q: Can you handle emergency roadside assistance?

Absolutely. We offer prompt roadside assistance for your convenience. Our team is available to help you with flat tires, jump-starts, and other common issues when you're on the road.
