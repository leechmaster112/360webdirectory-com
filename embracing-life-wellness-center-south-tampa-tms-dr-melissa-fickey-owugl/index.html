FAQ

1. What is TMS therapy and how is it performed at your South Tampa location?

TMS therapy is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain to improve symptoms of depression. At our clinic on W Kennedy Blvd, you will sit comfortably in a treatment chair. A magnetic coil is gently placed against your scalp, delivering brief pulses to the prefrontal cortex. The process is outpatient, meaning you can drive yourself to and from your appointments at our Tampa office.

2. How do I know if I am a candidate for TMS at your Tampa wellness center?

TMS therapy is typically for individuals who have struggled with Major Depressive Disorder and have not experienced satisfactory results from prior antidepressant medications. The best way to determine if you are a candidate is to schedule a consultation with Dr. Melissa Fickey at our South Tampa location. She will conduct a thorough assessment to see if TMS is the right treatment for you.

3. What can I expect during my first visit to your office at 3333 W Kennedy Blvd?

Your first visit to Embracing Life Wellness Center is a comprehensive consultation. You will meet with Dr. Fickey to discuss your mental health history, current challenges, and treatment goals. This appointment is designed to answer all of your questions and to determine the best course of action, which may include TMS therapy. Our team will ensure you feel informed and supported from the very start.

4. Are the TMS treatments at your clinic covered by insurance?

Many insurance plans do provide coverage for TMS therapy for treatment-resistant depression. Our administrative team at the South Tampa TMS center will work directly with your insurance provider to verify your benefits and explain any out-of-pocket costs before you begin your treatment regimen. We are committed to making this transformative care as accessible as possible.

5. How long does a full course of TMS treatment take at your Tampa facility?

A typical treatment course involves sessions five days a week for approximately 4-6 weeks, with each session lasting about 20-40 minutes. The total duration can vary based on your individual response to treatment. All treatments are conducted right here at our convenient Tampa location on Kennedy Blvd.
