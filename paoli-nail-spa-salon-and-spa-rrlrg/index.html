Professional Nail Care & Spa Treatments at Paoli Nail Spa

Discover the art of beautiful, well-cared-for nails right here in our Paoli Nail Spa salon. Located at the heart of Paoli Shopping Center on 27 Leopard Road, we are dedicated to providing exceptional nail care and spa services that transform your hands and feet into a reflection of your personal style and well-being.

At Paoli Nail Spa, we believe that beautiful nails should be an effortless luxury, not a complicated chore. Our skilled technicians combine expertise with a touch of pampering to deliver results that exceed expectations. Whether you desire a classic, chic, or avant-garde look, our range of services is designed to cater to your unique preferences.

Our Signature Services Include:

Nail Enhancements: Experience the transformative power of our nail extensions. From classic tips to innovative systems like dip powder and acrylic overlays, we offer various techniques to achieve the length and shape you desire. We also provide meticulous nail art, intricate designs, and vibrant gel manicures using top-quality polishes that withstand daily wear and tear.

Manicures & Pedicures: Indulge in our luxurious hot oil manicures and pedicures. These treatments not only beautify but also nourish and revitalize your skin. We utilize premium products to ensure your nails are healthy, strong, and your skin is moisturized and glowing. Expect attention to detail, from cuticle care to the final polish application.

Spa Foot Care: Pamper your feet with our specialized spa treatments. Our pedicures go beyond basic nail care, incorporating exfoliation, massage, and foot soak options to leave your feet feeling rejuvenated and healthy.

We are committed to using high-quality tools and products to ensure the health and safety of your nails. Our serene and welcoming environment allows you to relax and unwind while receiving top-notch service.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What services does Paoli Nail Spa offer?

A1: We specialize in nail enhancements (including acrylic, dip powder, tips, and nail art), classic and deluxe manicures, hot oil treatments, pedicures, and spa foot care services.

Q2: Do you offer nail extensions for both fingernails and toenails?

A2: Yes, our skilled technicians offer nail extension services for both fingernails and toenails using various techniques to achieve your desired look and length.

Q3: What should I expect during a manicure or pedicure?

A3: You can expect a relaxing experience including nail shaping, cuticle care, nail cleaning, polish application (or treatment for pedicures), and a thorough foot or hand massage. We offer hot oil treatments for added moisturizing benefits.

Q4: How long does a typical manicure or pedicure take?

A4: A standard manicure typically takes about 30 minutes, while a deluxe manicure or pedicure can take 45-60 minutes. Complex nail art designs may require additional time.

Q5: Do you provide nail care advice and maintenance tips?

A5: Absolutely! Our technicians are happy to provide advice on nail care, maintenance, and product recommendations to help you maintain your beautiful look between salon visits.
