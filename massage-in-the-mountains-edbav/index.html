Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What types of massage services do you offer at your Mt Pleasant location?

We offer a variety of massage modalities including Swedish, deep tissue, hot stone, and sports massage, among others. Our therapists can recommend the best option based on your specific needs.

Q: How long does a typical massage session last?

Typically, our massage sessions last between 30 and 90 minutes. We offer shorter and longer options to accommodate different schedules and desired levels of treatment.

Q: Do I need to book an appointment in advance?

We recommend booking your appointment ahead of time to ensure availability, especially during busy periods. Walk-ins are welcome if we have space, but we cannot guarantee immediate service.

Q: What should I expect during my first visit?

Upon your first visit, you'll complete an intake form detailing your health history and wellness goals. Then, you'll have a private consultation with your therapist before your massage begins. Afterwards, you'll have an opportunity to discuss your experience.

Q: Are gift certificates available for purchase?

Yes, we offer gift certificates for any amount. These are perfect for giving the ultimate relaxing gift to someone special in your life. They can be purchased online or in-store.
