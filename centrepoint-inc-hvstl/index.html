Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is hypnotherapy?

A1: Hypnotherapy is a therapeutic technique that uses guided relaxation, intense concentration, and focused attention to achieve a heightened state of awareness, often compared to daydreaming, in which subjects are more open to suggestion. At CentrePoint Inc, we use this state to help you access your subconscious mind for healing and positive change.

Q2: Is hypnosis safe?

A2: Yes, hypnosis is a safe and natural state. At CentrePoint Inc, our practitioners are trained professionals who ensure your experience is comfortable and beneficial. You remain in control throughout the session.

Q3: Will I be asleep during hypnosis?

A3: No, hypnosis is a state of deep relaxation and heightened awareness, not sleep. You are fully aware of your surroundings and can respond to the practitioner if needed.
