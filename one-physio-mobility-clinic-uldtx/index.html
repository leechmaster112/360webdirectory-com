Location

Welcome to One Physio & Mobility Clinic, your dedicated partner in regaining function, reducing pain, and improving your quality of life. Located at 160 96 Ave NE Unit 105, Calgary, AB T3K 6G4, we are proud to serve the residents of Harvest Hills and surrounding areas with our premier physiotherapy and mobility services.

At One Physio & Mobility, we understand that pain and limited mobility can significantly impact your daily activities, work, and overall well-being. Our mission is simple: to restore your ability to move freely and confidently. We achieve this through personalized care, expert assessment, and evidence-based treatment plans tailored specifically to your unique needs and goals.

Our Services

Our team of experienced and compassionate physiotherapists offers a comprehensive range of services designed to address various musculoskeletal conditions and injuries:

Acute Injury Management: We provide prompt and effective treatment for sprains, strains, fractures, post-operative rehabilitation, and other acute injuries to help you recover faster and safely return to your activities.

Chronic Pain Management: We specialize in developing long-term strategies to manage persistent pain, utilizing techniques like manual therapy, therapeutic exercises, and movement re-education to reduce discomfort and improve function.

Mobility Restoration: Our focus on mobility is central to our practice. We employ specialized techniques to improve joint range of motion, enhance flexibility, and restore functional movement patterns essential for everyday life.

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation: We design individualized rehabilitation programs following orthopedic surgeries, helping you regain strength, stability, and confidence in your recovery journey.

Neurological Rehabilitation: Our therapists are trained to assist patients recovering from neurological conditions, focusing on improving balance, coordination, gait, and functional independence.

Workplace and Ergonomic Assessments: We can help identify potential risk factors in your work environment and recommend modifications to prevent injury and promote well-being.

Personalized Exercise Programming: We create customized exercise plans to complement your treatment, focusing on strengthening, flexibility, and functional movement to support your recovery and long-term health.

Our Approach

We believe in a patient-centered approach. Your journey begins with a thorough assessment to accurately diagnose the issue and understand your goals. Treatment is not just about alleviating symptoms; it's about addressing the underlying causes and empowering you with knowledge and tools for self-management.

Our therapists are committed to staying at the forefront of physiotherapy practice, ensuring you receive evidence-based care using the latest techniques and technologies.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What conditions do you treat at One Physio & Mobility Clinic?

We treat a wide range of musculoskeletal conditions, including back and neck pain, shoulder and knee injuries, hip and ankle problems, arthritis, sports injuries, post-operative rehabilitation, chronic pain, mobility issues, and conditions related to neurological rehabilitation. Please call us or visit our website to discuss your specific condition.

How long does a typical physiotherapy treatment take?

The duration of your initial assessment and treatment session can vary depending on the complexity of your condition and the extent of your injuries. Typically, the first visit lasts around 60-90 minutes to allow ample time for a comprehensive evaluation and initial treatment plan discussion. Follow-up appointments usually range from 30-60 minutes.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring your valid health card information, any relevant medical reports or diagnostic imaging (X-rays, MRIs, CT scans) if applicable, a list of current medications, and your insurance information if you plan to use your extended health benefits for coverage. Wear comfortable clothing that allows easy access to the area being treated.

Do you accept insurance?

One Physio & Mobility Clinic accepts direct billing for patients with most extended health insurance plans. Please contact our office at (403) 984-1669 to confirm your specific plan's coverage details and pre-authorization requirements before your appointment.

What is your cancellation policy?

We understand that unexpected circumstances may arise. Please provide at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment. Failure to do so may result in a charge for the missed appointment.
