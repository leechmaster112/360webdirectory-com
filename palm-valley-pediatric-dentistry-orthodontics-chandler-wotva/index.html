Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What age do you start seeing children?

We welcome children starting from infancy. Our practice offers specialized care for newborns through adolescents.

Q: How often should my child see the dentist?

We generally recommend dental check-ups every six months for most children. However, the frequency may vary depending on individual needs.

Q: Do you treat dental anxiety in children?

Absolutely. We specialize in managing dental anxiety in children. Our team uses techniques like positive reinforcement, distraction, and a gentle approach to ensure a comfortable experience.

Q: When should my child first see an orthodontist?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends an initial orthodontic evaluation by age 7. We offer convenient orthodontic screenings at our Chandler location.

Q: Do you offer emergency dental services?

Yes, we understand dental emergencies can happen at any time. We strive to accommodate urgent dental needs for our Chandler patients.
