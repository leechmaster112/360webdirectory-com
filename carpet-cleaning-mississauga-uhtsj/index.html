Q1: What types of commercial carpets do you clean?

We clean a wide range of commercial carpets, including but not limited to Berber, cut pile, loop pile, and textured commercial carpets. Our techniques and solutions are tailored to the specific type of carpet to ensure the best results without causing damage.

Q2: How long does a typical commercial carpet cleaning job take?

The duration depends on the size of the area, the level of soiling, and the specific cleaning required. Generally, a standard commercial carpet cleaning job can take anywhere from 2 to 6 hours. We will provide you with a precise estimate after inspecting your carpets.

Q3: Do you offer any guarantees on your cleaning services?

Yes, we stand behind the quality of our work. We offer a satisfaction guarantee on our commercial carpet cleaning services. If you are not completely satisfied with the results, please contact us, and we will address any concerns promptly.

Q4: How often should commercial carpets be professionally cleaned?

It is generally recommended to have commercial carpets professionally cleaned at least once or twice a year, depending on foot traffic and usage. High-traffic areas may require more frequent cleaning. We can advise you on the best cleaning schedule based on your specific needs.
