Location

Welcome to Driftwood Bar & Grill, your destination for relaxed, quality dining and drinks in welcoming environments across Michigan and New York.

At Driftwood Bar & Grill, we pride ourselves on offering a genuinely casual dining experience focused on classic American flavors. Whether you're grabbing a quick lunch, enjoying a leisurely brunch, or unwinding with friends and family over cocktails or a full dinner, our atmosphere is designed to be comfortable and inviting.

Brunch Delights

Start your weekend right or refuel after a night out with our delicious brunch menu. We offer a selection of hearty staples and lighter bites, perfect for sharing or enjoying solo. From fluffy omelets and crispy hash browns to satisfying pancakes and savory breakfast burritos, we've got options to satisfy any brunch craving. Don't forget to pair your brunch with our popular happy hour specials, often featuring discounted cocktails and appetizers.

Lunchtime Favorites

Looking for a satisfying meal during the day? Our lunch menu provides a range of options, from classic sandwiches and salads to lighter fare like soups and wraps. Crafted with quality ingredients, our lunch dishes are perfect for a quick workday bite or a more substantial meal out. Enjoy the relaxed vibe while you eat, making your lunch break something to look forward to.

Bar & Grill Offerings

Our bar menu is always a highlight, featuring an array of handcrafted cocktails, premium beers on tap and in bottles, a carefully curated selection of wines, and classic spirits. Whether you're in the mood for a signature drink, a local craft beer, or a fine wine pairing, our knowledgeable staff is happy to help you find the perfect sip. For lighter appetizers or sharing, our bar snacks are always available.

Experience the heart of our establishments during happy hour, typically offering discounted drinks and specially priced small plates, providing great value for a relaxed gathering.
