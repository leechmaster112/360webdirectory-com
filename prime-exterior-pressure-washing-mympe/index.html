Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is pressure washing, and how does it work?

Pressure washing uses a high-powered water jet to blast away dirt, grime, mold, mildew, and loose paint from surfaces like driveways, decks, and siding. Our advanced equipment delivers the right pressure and technique to clean effectively without damaging the surface.

What surfaces can you pressure wash?

We are experienced in pressure washing a wide variety of surfaces, including concrete, asphalt, wood decks and siding, vinyl fencing, brick, and more. We always assess the surface type and adjust our pressure and techniques accordingly.

Do you offer services other than pressure washing?

Absolutely! In addition to our core pressure washing services, we also specialize in gutter cleaning, roof washing, deck staining and sealing, window washing, and comprehensive exterior cleaning and detailing for residential and commercial properties in Orlando.

How do I prepare my property for exterior cleaning?

Generally, we ask to clear the immediate area around the surface being cleaned (like your driveway or deck) of potted plants, furniture, toys, and other items. This ensures their safety and allows us better access. For specific preparation instructions, we'll advise you during our consultation.

Do you offer estimates and are you licensed?

Yes, we provide free, no-obligation estimates for all our services. You can contact us via phone at 407-284-3387 or through our website at https://www.primeexteriorpressurewashing.com/ to schedule a consultation. We are fully insured and committed to providing professional, reliable service.
