Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is the main service offered at Chubby Cheeks Ultrasound Studio in Pittsburgh?

A1: The primary service offered at our Pittsburgh location is advanced 3D and 4D ultrasound imaging for expectant parents.

Q2: How does 4D ultrasound differ from 3D ultrasound?

A2: 3D ultrasound provides a still, three-dimensional image of the baby. 4D ultrasound adds the element of time, showing the baby's movements in real-time.

Q3: Is the 3D/4D ultrasound safe for the baby?

A3: Yes, the 3D and 4D ultrasound examinations performed at Chubby Cheeks are considered safe. The intensity of the sound waves used is well within the guidelines established by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and the American Institute of Ultrasound in Medicine (AIUM) for obstetrical imaging.

Q4: How early can I get a 3D/4D ultrasound at your Pittsburgh studio?

A4: A 3D or 4D ultrasound can typically be performed starting around week 14 of pregnancy. The optimal time for detailed imaging is often between weeks 26 and 34, but we can accommodate requests before and after this window, subject to image quality and fetal position.

Q5: Do I need a doctor's prescription for a 3D/4D ultrasound in Pittsburgh?

A5: While a standard pregnancy can be scanned without a prescription, we require a valid prescription from your obstetrician-gynecologist (OBGYN) or midwife for medically indicated ultrasounds. For purely elective 3D/4D scans, a doctor's note confirming the pregnancy is typically sufficient.
