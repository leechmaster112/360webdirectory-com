Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What should I expect during my first tattoo appointment?

A1: Your first visit includes a consultation where our artists discuss design ideas and placement. We'll determine the best approach for your chosen style and size. We'll also explain the tattooing process and aftercare instructions to ensure you're fully prepared.

Q2: How long does a typical tattoo session last?

A2: Session length varies based on tattoo size and complexity. Most standard tattoos take 1-3 hours, while larger or more detailed pieces may require multiple sessions. Our artists will provide an estimated timeline during your consultation.

Q3: What forms of payment do you accept?

A3: We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and cash. A deposit is required for larger projects, which will be applied to your final balance.

Q4: Do you offer touch-ups or cover-ups?

A4: Yes, we provide complimentary touch-up services within the first year after your initial tattoo. We also specialize in cover-up tattoos, transforming existing designs into new, meaningful artwork.

Q5: What is your cancellation policy?

A5: We kindly request at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations. Failure to cancel with proper notice may result in forfeiture of your deposit.
