Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What areas do you treat for laser hair removal?

A: At our Barrie location, we offer laser hair removal for virtually all body parts, including the face, legs, arms, back, and bikini line.

Q2: How many sessions of microneedling are typically needed?

A: The number of microneedling sessions required depends on your individual skin concerns and desired results. Generally, a series of 3-6 sessions spaced 4-6 weeks apart is recommended for optimal outcomes in Barrie.

Q3: Is IPL suitable for all skin tones?

A: IPL technology is effective for various skin tones, but results can vary. Our Barrie team will assess your skin type during a consultation to determine if IPL is the right choice for you.

Q4: How long does a typical laser treatment session take?

A: Treatment times vary depending on the area being treated. Facial laser procedures might take 15-30 minutes, while larger body areas like the legs could require 30-60 minutes at our clinic.

Q5: What is the downtime after a laser treatment?

A: Downtime varies by treatment. Laser hair removal may cause mild redness, while microneedling and IPL might result in redness, swelling, or a sunburn-like sensation. Our Barrie team will provide detailed post-treatment care instructions.
