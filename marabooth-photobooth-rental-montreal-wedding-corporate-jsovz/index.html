Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Do you offer a no-time-limit rental for your photobooths?

A: Yes, absolutely! At Marabooth, we understand that events vary in length, and you shouldn't worry about exceeding a rental time. Our pricing structure is designed for flexibility. No time limit on your event! You pay one fixed price for the rental, and you can enjoy our photobooth services for as long as you need, ensuring maximum guest enjoyment without unexpected charges.

Q2: What types of events do you typically provide photobooth rentals for?

A: We specialize in a wide range of events! Our photobooths are perfect for weddings, corporate functions (like product launches, conferences, and holiday parties), birthdays, social gatherings, graduations, proms, festivals, and market stalls. Essentially, if you have an event where you want to create fun, interactive moments and capture memories, we can help!

Q3: How does the photobooth work? Do guests need technical skills?

A: Our digital photobooths are incredibly user-friendly! Guests simply insert their bill (or use our contactless payment option if enabled) into the ticket dispenser, select a duration (usually 15 or 30 seconds), and the booth automatically starts. They can then pose using the touch-screen interface, choose from various flash modes and effects, and take a picture. The booth will automatically capture a great shot (or offer a re-take if needed) and print it out for them shortly after. No technical skills required!

Q4: What is included in your rental package?

A: Our standard rental package includes the photobooth unit itself, a selection of fun props, the ticket dispenser, printing paper, and our setup, operation, and basic breakdown services. We also provide a digital gallery access link for guests to view and share their photos online. Specific inclusions can vary by package, so feel free to inquire about the details that best suit your event.

Q5: Do you travel outside of Montreal?

A: Yes, we do! While our primary base is Montreal, we are happy to travel to other locations within the province of Quebec and also serve clients in New York, NY, as listed in our address. Please contact us via phone or our website to discuss travel arrangements and potential additional fees for events outside our immediate service area.
