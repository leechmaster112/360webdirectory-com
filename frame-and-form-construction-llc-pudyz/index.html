Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What exactly does Frame and Form Construction LLC do?

We specialize in the construction of concrete forms (shoring) and structural framing. We build the temporary structures that hold concrete in place while it cures, and we also handle the framing components that provide structural support.

Q2: Do you only work on commercial projects in Buffalo?

No, we serve a variety of clients in Buffalo, NY, including homeowners building custom residences, contractors working on retail stores, restaurants, warehouses, and other commercial properties.

Q3: How long does a typical concrete pouring project take?

The timeline varies significantly based on the project size, complexity of the forms, weather conditions, and the amount of concrete involved. We work closely with our clients to establish realistic schedules.

Q4: What types of concrete forms do you construct?

We handle a wide range of formwork, including but not limited to: standard wall forms, footing forms, footing wall forms, pile cap forms, special-shaped architectural forms, and complex basement or foundation wall systems common in Buffalo area construction.

Q5: Are you licensed and insured to work in Buffalo?

Yes, Frame and Form Construction LLC operates with all necessary local licenses and permits required for construction work in Buffalo, NY. We also maintain comprehensive liability insurance and worker's compensation coverage to protect our clients and our team.
