Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of sewer line problems do you repair?

We handle a variety of issues, including clogs and blockages, pipe cracks and breaks, tree root intrusion, pipe misalignment, slow drains, gurgling sounds, and sewage backups originating from the main sewer line.

Q2: How quickly can you respond to an emergency sewer line repair?

We understand that sewer line emergencies require immediate attention. While response times can vary based on the nature of the emergency and our technician availability, we prioritize urgent calls and aim to dispatch help as quickly as possible, often within a short timeframe.

Q3: Do you offer camera inspections to diagnose sewer line problems?

Yes, we utilize advanced sewer camera inspection technology at our Springfield location. This allows us to visually inspect the interior of your sewer lines to accurately identify the location and extent of any damage or blockage without unnecessary digging.

Q4: What is the typical cost for a sewer line repair?

The cost of sewer line repair varies significantly depending on the specific issue, the location of the problem within your line, the type of pipe material, and the extent of the damage. We provide a detailed estimate before starting any work. For an accurate assessment of your situation, we recommend scheduling a consultation.

Q5: Are your sewer line repair services only for residential properties?

No, while we serve many residential customers in Springfield, our services also cater to businesses and property owners who require sewer line repair or maintenance. Our team is equipped to handle sewer line issues of various sizes and complexities.
