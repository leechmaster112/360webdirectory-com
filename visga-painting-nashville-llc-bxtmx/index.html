Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How long does a typical house painting project take?

A: The duration of a house painting project depends on factors like the size of the home, the extent of prep work needed, and the number of coats. On average, an exterior project might take 3-7 days, while an interior project could range from 2-5 days. We provide a detailed timeline during your consultation.

Q2: What type of paint do you use?

A: We primarily use high-quality paints from reputable brands like Benjamin Moore, Sherwin-Williams, and Behr. The specific type (latex, oil-based, etc.) and color will depend on your project's requirements and preferences, which we discuss during the consultation.

Q3: Do you handle the preparation work?

A: Yes, thorough preparation is essential for a great paint job. Our team handles all aspects of preparation, including cleaning, scraping, sanding, priming, and protecting your home's surfaces and interior.

Q4: Can you match a specific paint color?

A: Absolutely! We have access to extensive paint color charts and can match virtually any color sample you provide. We can also help you select a color that complements your home's style and enhances its appearance.

Q5: What's the best time of year for exterior painting?

A: The best times for exterior painting in Nashville are typically during the late spring, summer, and early fall when temperatures are moderate and humidity is lower. This allows the paint to dry properly and adhere well. We can advise you on the optimal timing based on your specific project.
