Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Do I need prior experience to try axe throwing at your Toms River location?

A: Absolutely not! We welcome beginners and experienced individuals alike. Our staff will provide a safety briefing and basic instructions to get you started.

Q2: What is the minimum age requirement for participants at Axe Throwing Toms River NJ?

A: Guests must be at least 18 years old to participate independently. Minors must be accompanied by a responsible adult who is also participating.

Q3: Is axe throwing safe? What safety measures do you have in place at your facility?

A: Safety is our primary concern. We use high-quality, blunt-tipped axes and well-padded targets. Strict rules are enforced, including mandatory eye and head protection, designated throwing areas, and limits on speed and force. Our trained staff supervise all sessions.

Q4: Can I book a private event or group session exclusively at your Toms River location?

A: Yes, we offer private booking options for groups. You can reserve the entire lane or the entire venue for your event. Please contact us using our website or phone number for details and availability.

Q5: What should I wear to an axe throwing session at 1436 Hooper Ave?

A: Wear comfortable clothing and sturdy, closed-toe shoes. Avoid loose clothing or jewelry that could interfere with your throw or get caught. We provide all necessary throwing gear.
