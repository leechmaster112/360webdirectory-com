Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What axe throwing styles do you offer?

We primarily offer competitive style axe throwing, focusing on accuracy and precision. Guests can participate in individual challenges or team-based competitions.

Q2: Can I book a private event at your Denver location?

Absolutely! We host private parties, corporate events, and team-building sessions right here at our 1512 Larimer St location. Please visit our website for booking details.

Q3: What are your hours of operation?

We are open Tuesday through Sunday, typically from 4:00 PM to 11:00 PM. Please check our website for the most current schedule.

Q4: Do you provide axes for beginners?

Yes, we provide all necessary equipment, including proper-sized and weighted axes for beginners. Our trained staff will guide you through the basics.

Q5: What is the minimum age for axe throwing?

For safety reasons, guests must be at least 18 years old to participate in axe throwing at our Denver location.
