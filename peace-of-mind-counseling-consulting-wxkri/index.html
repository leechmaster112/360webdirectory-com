Peace of Mind Counseling & Consulting

Peace of Mind Counseling & Consulting (POMCC) is a premier mental health practice serving Freehold, NJ, and the surrounding communities, with over 10 years of dedicated experience helping individuals, couples, and families overcome life's emotional challenges. Located at 63 W Main Street, Suite A-5, in Freehold, POMCC provides both in-person counseling services and convenient telehealth options for clients throughout New Jersey. For those ready to prioritize their mental health and unlock a brighter, more fulfilling future, POMCC invites you to schedule an initial consultation by calling (848) 300-3933 or visiting their welcoming office in downtown Freehold. The practice specializes in evidence-based treatment for anxiety, depression, trauma, anger management, relationship issues, and life transitions, employing a collaborative and holistic approach that addresses the physical, emotional, and spiritual aspects of each client's wellbeing. POMCC's team of licensed counselors utilizes proven therapeutic modalities including Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Rational Emotional Behavioral Therapy (REBT), Dialectical Behavioral Therapy (DBT), Internal Family Systems, and Eye Movement Desensitization & Reprocessing (EMDR) to deliver efficient, effective care. The practice works exclusively with adults 18 and older, offering individualized treatment plans that not only address immediate symptoms but also explore underlying patterns to create lasting change. Whether clients are facing a new crisis or struggling with longstanding emotional patterns, POMCC's direct and thoughtful approach helps individuals develop insight, acquire new coping skills, and relieve distress in a judgment-free, supportive environment. As a forward-thinking practice committed to both client wellness and environmental responsibility, Peace of Mind Counseling & Consulting has partnered with Plantd to offset their carbon footprint, ensuring that each therapeutic journey contributes to a healthier planet for future generations. The practice offers flexible appointment times and multiple ways to connect with therapists, recognizing that finding the right therapeutic fit is paramount to successful treatment.
