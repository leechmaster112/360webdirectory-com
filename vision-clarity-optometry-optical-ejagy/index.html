Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How often should I get an eye exam at Vision Clarity Optometry & Optical?

A: We recommend getting a comprehensive eye exam at least once a year, even if you don't notice any changes in your vision. Regular check-ups help detect eye diseases early and ensure your prescription is up-to-date.

Q2: Does Vision Clarity Optometry & Optical accept insurance?

A: Yes, we accept most private insurance plans. Please bring your insurance card with you to your appointment, and our staff will assist with the necessary paperwork.

Q3: What types of lenses do you offer for eyeglasses?

A: We offer a wide range of lens options, including single vision, bifocals, progressives, anti-glare coatings, blue light filters, photochromic lenses, and high-definition single vision lenses.

Q4: Can you adjust my existing eyewear at your Mississauga location?

A: Absolutely! Our optical team is experienced in adjusting frames for comfort and proper fit. We can make minor adjustments to ensure your glasses are comfortable and provide optimal vision.

Q5: Do you offer contact lens fittings?

A: Yes, our optometrists provide thorough contact lens fittings and consultations. We ensure that your contact lenses are the right prescription, material, and type for your lifestyle and visual needs.
