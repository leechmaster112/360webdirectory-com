Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Is the 3D/4D ultrasound safe? A: Yes, the 3D/4D ultrasound technology used at Chubby Cheeks 3D/4D Ultrasound Studio of Pittsburgh operates at the same safe frequency as the standard 2D ultrasound used in all prenatal care. The American Institute of Ultrasound in Medicine (AIUM) and the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) have stated that ultrasound is safe when used appropriately by trained professionals.

Q2: How long does a 3D/4D ultrasound session take? A: A typical 3D/4D ultrasound session at our Pittsburgh studio lasts approximately 45 minutes to an hour. This includes the time for the technologist to gather clear images and for you to ask questions. The actual scanning time is usually shorter.

Q3: How early can I get a 3D/4D ultrasound at your Pittsburgh studio? A: Generally, 3D/4D ultrasounds are best performed between 13 and 34 weeks of pregnancy. This is when the baby has developed enough facial features for clear imaging but still has sufficient amniotic fluid for good visualization. We recommend booking your appointment around 14-16 weeks.

Q4: Do I need a prescription or referral for a 3D/4D ultrasound at Chubby Cheeks? A: No, 3D/4D ultrasounds are considered an elective service and do not require a prescription or referral from your doctor. However, we always encourage you to share any pregnancy information with us to help us capture the best images.

Q5: Can I bring photos or videos of my baby after the ultrasound? A: Absolutely! While we capture amazing images and videos during your session, we encourage you to continue the connection with your baby by taking photos or videos of them after they are born. We are happy to help you remember this special moment.
